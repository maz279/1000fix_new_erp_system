Part 1 · Getting started

1.1 Log in

  1. Open the ERP address in any modern browser — desktop or phone, the shell adapts.
  2. Enter your work email and password. First-time users receive credentials from IT (module M66).
  3. Type the 6-digit code from your authenticator app — the box auto-advances digit by digit.
  4. You land on your Role Centre — a home page assembled for your job.
Never share your login. Accounts are personal, MFA-protected, and every action is logged to you (module M29). Colleagues who need access get their own account — ask IT.

1.3 The mega menu — one click to any screen

  1. Click an area (e.g. Service Operations) in the left navigation.
  2. The mega menu lists every module's screen groups — click any screen to open it.
  3. The menu closes automatically when you navigate; click outside to dismiss it without choosing.

1.4 Tell-ME — finding anything in seconds

Try it now: Alt+Q, type dispatch, press ↓ twice, then Enter. You are on the dispatch board. This one habit replaces most navigation.

1.5 Make the system yours

  • Language — the বাংলা/EN button switches the shell instantly; modules and screens carry Bangla names too.
  • Density — compact / standard / relaxed changes table row height (cashiers love compact).
  • Dark mode — remembered per device; charts are tuned for both themes.
  • Phone use — on a small screen the left rail collapses; the ☰ button opens it as an overlay that closes when you navigate.
