## 1. Purpose and Method

This audit is **mandatory under the commissioning instruction** and was performed immediately after
the nine Category 07 documents were issued, before any consumer relied on them.

It applies the four methods established by `PGD-AUDIT-001`:

| # | Method | What was checked |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Structural consistency** | Front-matter completeness against `FOLDER_STRUCTURE.md` §6; identifier uniqueness and gap-freedom in every reserved space; link integrity; code-space collisions across all six repositories |
| 2 | **Arithmetic consistency** | Every roll-up regenerated by counting its source rows immediately before issue (`PG-M10`) |
| 3 | **External verification** | Every count, keyword and absence claim re-executed against the filesystem; every vendor version, licence and lifecycle claim re-verified against upstream on 2026-08-18 |
| 4 | **Temporal verification** | Whether the estate changed between first enumeration and issue (`PG-M11`) |

**Result: 4 critical, 5 high, 8 medium and 7 low findings — 24 in total. Eleven are closed.
Thirteen remain open.**

**Seven of the twenty-four are against Category 07's own work** — one inherited count repeated
without recounting (`BEA-19`), one roll-up that did not sum to its own rows (`BEA-23`), one headline
figure that contradicted its own section (`BEA-24`), one worked example that allocated a live
identifier (`BEA-25`), one finding allocated two identifiers (`BEA-22`), one audit-identifier
collision avoided rather than committed (`BEA-20`), and one register staleness this session chose not
to fix (`BEA-21`).

Each is recorded rather than smoothed over, on the principle `PGD-AUDIT-001` established: a category
whose own error history is hidden cannot ask the rest of the programme to disclose theirs.

> **`BEA-19` is the one worth reading first.** `BE-002` §1 opened by stating that *"coding standard"*
> occurs **four times** in the source estate. That figure came from `PGD-000` and
> `_CATEGORY_OVERVIEW.md`. It was not counted. The actual figure is **seven occurrences across four
> files**. The document that cites `PG-03` — *count, do not consult* — as its governing rule breached

## `BEA-04` — There is no configuration effort anywhere in the estate

**Severity: Critical · Type: Estimate completeness · Status: OPEN**

Two independent readings of the same 79 files:

| Source | Method | Result |
|---|---|---:|
| `GV-007` §4.1 | Sum of §1 **Effort estimate** | **1,842 PW** |
| `BE-005` §6 | Sum of §16.2 **custom-development** rows | **1,850 PW** |

`02-05` §1 and TSS §10.14 both state that **Odoo 19 CE covers ~28% of requirements natively and OCA

**If the §1 effort figure equals the custom-development sum for 95% of units, no line anywhere in the
estate carries L1 and L2 configuration effort.**

| Reading | Consequence |
|---|---|
| (a) The §1 figure was only ever a custom-development figure | Configuration effort is **entirely unbudgeted**, by an unmeasured amount |
| (b) Configuration is silently inside the CD rows | The CD rows are not custom-development estimates, and every figure derived from them measures something other than what it names |

`GV-007` §7.3 lists seven explicitly excluded work streams. Configuration is not among them, so it is
excluded **by accident rather than by decision** — which is the distinction that matters, because an
accidental exclusion is never funded.

**Owner:** CTO / `GV-007`. **Needed by:** W0 — blocking, because it invalidates every sprint estimate
downstream of it (`BE-008` `Q-07`).

## `BEA-20` — The audit identifier space, and a collision avoided rather than committed

**Severity: Medium · Type: Identifier governance · Status: CLOSED with residual**

`PGD-AUDIT-nnn` is a **sequential space with no allocating authority**. `RQA-34` recorded two
sessions taking `PGD-AUDIT-002` eight minutes apart; `PGD-A2-27` recommended a single authority;
`RR-002` was asked to re-base the space to a category-keyed form. None of the three was executed.

At 19:48 (+06) the space held `001`–`005`, with `005` taken by Category 03 — while Categories 04, 05,
06 and 07 were all being authored concurrently. Taking `006` would have made this audit the fifth
claimant on a value four other sessions could compute identically.

**This audit therefore adopts `PGD-AUDIT-CAT-07`**, derivable from the category alone and computable
without reference to any other session's work. `PGD-AUDIT-001`…`005` are permanent and are **not**
renumbered (`RG-02`); the two forms coexist until `RR-002` rules. Recorded as rule **`PG-M14`** in
`FOLDER_STRUCTURE.md` §7.

**The decision was vindicated within two hours, twice.** At 19:48 the sequential space held
`001`–`005` and `006` was free. By 21:59 two further audits had been issued: **`PGD-AUDIT-006`** for
Category **06**, and **`PGD-AUDIT-007`** for Category **04**. Had this audit taken the next free
number at 19:48 it would have collided with Category 06.

**And the space is now actively misleading.** `PGD-AUDIT-007` denotes **Category 04**, not
Category 07. A reader who assumes the number tracks the category — which is the only reason a reader
would ever look at the number — is wrong. That is not a hypothetical risk in a sequential space; it
is what a sequential space does as soon as more than one thing allocates from it.

**Residual:** the sequential space still exists and can still collide for anyone who uses it.
`RR-002` remains unwritten after four recommendations.

> This is the same fix `BE-005` applied to the `CD` space, for the same reason. A scheme whose next
> value is a function of a moving baseline **cannot** survive concurrent authoring; a derivable
> scheme needs no allocator and therefore cannot fail this way. It is the single most reusable
> lesson in this estate and it has now been learned five times.

## `BEA-10` — Two manifest version formats, one of which is in an architecture standard

**Severity: Medium · Status: OPEN**

`AR-007` §3.2's manifest example annotates `"19.0.1.0.0"` as
`<odoo>.<major>.<minor>.<patch>.<build>` — five components with a build number.

OCA's contributing conventions define the module version as
`{odoo_version}.{major}.{minor}.{patch}` — **four** components, in which `odoo_version` is the
two-part series `19.0`. There is no build component, and Odoo's own update mechanism reads the
four-part form.

`BE-002` §4.6's `pylint-odoo` configuration implements the OCA reading with
`manifest_version_format = ^19\.0\.\d+\.\d+\.\d+$`, and `BE-003` `GW-31` follows it. **The
architecture standard's annotation should be corrected in `AR-007` v1.1** so that the two documents
do not teach different things to the same developer.
