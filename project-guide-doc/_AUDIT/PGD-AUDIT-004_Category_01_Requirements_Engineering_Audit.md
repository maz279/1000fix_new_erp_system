## 1. Purpose and Method

This audit is **mandatory under the commissioning instruction** and was performed immediately
after the ten Category 01 documents were issued, before any consumer relied on them.

It applies the four methods established by `PGD-AUDIT-001`:

| # | Method | What was checked |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Structural consistency** | Front-matter completeness against `FOLDER_STRUCTURE.md` §6; identifier uniqueness and gap-freedom in every reserved space; link integrity; code-space collisions across all five repositories |
| 2 | **Arithmetic consistency** | Every roll-up regenerated by counting its source rows immediately before issue (`PG-M10`) |
| 3 | **External verification** | Every count, keyword and absence claim re-executed against the filesystem |
| 4 | **Temporal verification** | Whether the estate changed between first enumeration and issue (`PG-M11`) |

**Result: 5 critical, 13 high, 12 medium and 4 low findings — 34 in total.
Thirteen are closed. Twenty-one remain open.**

**Three of the thirty-four are against Category 01's own work** — one arithmetic error in a
roll-up (`RQA-32`), one absence asserted from an incomplete search (`RQA-33`), and one identifier
collision inherited at allocation (`RQA-07`). A fourth (`RQA-34`) was committed by two concurrent
sessions and found by this audit's own filing.

Each is recorded rather than smoothed over, on the principle `PGD-AUDIT-001` established: a
category whose own error history is hidden cannot ask the rest of the programme to disclose
theirs.

# 3. Critical Findings

## RQA-02 — Zero of 3,347 requirement rows is a well-formed requirement

**Severity: Critical · Type: Requirement quality · Status: OPEN**

ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018 §5.2.4 requires a requirement to state a subject and an obligation, using
the modal **`shall`**. Executed at 2026-08-18 03:04 (+06):

```
grep -h -E '^\| *FR-' 03_Modules/*/[MP]*.md | grep -ciE '\bshall\b'   → 0
```

**Zero of 3,347 requirement rows contains the word.** The modal lives in the *column header* —
`Function — "The system shall…"` — and only **78 of 431** requirement tables carry even that; 346
use a bare `Function` header and 7 replace `Layer` with `Wave`.

**Consequence.** No row in the estate is a requirement in the sense the standard, a contract or a
test derivation uses. They are well-written **phrases**. `CO-003` cannot accept against a phrase,
`QA-003` cannot derive a technique from one, and a vendor pricing against 3,347 of them is pricing
against interpretation.

**Assessment, stated fairly.** On the *lexical* criteria of §5.2.7 the estate performs unusually
well: 17 loophole and subjective terms in 3,347 rows is 0.5%. The defect is structural, not
editorial, and structural defects convert mechanically. That is the basis of the conversion
procedure in `RQ-001` §3.10.7.

**Remediation.** `RQ-001` §3.10.2 makes the construct mandatory; §3.10.7 states the five-step
conversion with a worked example; §3.10.10 publishes checks `CK-01`–`CK-12`. **Open until the
conversion completes at the Wave 1 gate.**

## RQA-14 — The cost model rests on a superseded baseline

**Severity: Critical · Type: Arithmetic / stale basis · Status: OPEN**


**1,180 is the 30-module total that `05-01` §1.3 marks *"(30-module basis — superseded)"* on its

**Consequence.** The percentages `02-05` §1 publishes — L1 28%, L2 24%, L5 25% — are that
distribution's percentages. The **834 PW** effort figure, the **$455K–780K** cost band and every

**Remediation.** `RQ-003` §5.2 records it; §7 supplies the measured inputs `GV-007` needs to
restate it; rule `FG-09` forbids quoting a wave cost without its `undetermined` count. **Open —
closure belongs to `GV-007` and `CO-005`.**

## RQA-34 — Two audits shared the identifier `PGD-AUDIT-002`

**Severity: High · Type: Identifier collision · Status: CLOSED — resolved by the authoring
session during this audit; the control recommendation stands**

Enumerated at **2026-08-18 03:05 (+06)**:

| File | `id:` | Written |
|---|---|---|
| `_AUDIT/PGD-AUDIT-002_Category_00_Governance_Audit.md` | `PGD-AUDIT-002` | 03:05 |
| `_AUDIT/PGD-AUDIT-002_Category_02_Architecture_and_Design_Audit.md` | `PGD-AUDIT-002` | 03:03 |

**Re-enumerated at 03:14 (+06) — the collision no longer reproduces:**

| File | `id:` |
|---|---|
| `PGD-AUDIT-001_Register_Integrity_Audit.md` | `PGD-AUDIT-001` |
| `PGD-AUDIT-002_Category_00_Governance_Audit.md` | `PGD-AUDIT-002` |
| `PGD-AUDIT-003_Category_02_Architecture_and_Design_Audit.md` | **`PGD-AUDIT-003`** |
| `PGD-AUDIT-004_Category_01_Requirements_Engineering_Audit.md` | `PGD-AUDIT-004` |

The Category 02 session renamed its audit to `003` between 03:05 and 03:14 — after this audit
observed the collision and before it issued. The space is now clean.

**The finding is recorded, not deleted.** It was real for at least eleven minutes, it was found by
enumeration rather than by assertion, and the mechanism that produced it is unchanged. Deleting a
finding because the condition resolved itself would leave the register asserting that concurrent
sequential allocation is safe, which this occurrence disproves. It is closed on evidence, with the
control recommendation standing.

Two different audits, two different scopes, one identifier — in the repository whose own first
audit recorded identifier collision as the estate's largest defect class, and which published rule
`PG-M10` and the five-point issue checklist in response.

**Root cause.** `PGD-AUDIT-nnn` is a **sequential** space with no allocating authority. Three
sessions authored Categories 00, 01 and 02 concurrently on the same date; each computed "the next
number" from a filesystem that did not yet contain the others' work. Sequential allocation is
unsafe under concurrent authoring **by construction** — the next free number is a function of a
baseline that is moving.

**Action taken here.** This audit did **not** take `PGD-AUDIT-003`, which would have made the
collision harder to resolve by adding a third claimant adjacent to it. It took **`PGD-AUDIT-004`**,
leaving `002` and `003` free for the two colliding audits to resolve between themselves — which is
exactly what then happened.

**Remediation still recommended — `R-01`.** The instance resolved; the mechanism did not. Re-base the space to a **category-keyed** form:
`PGD-AUDIT-CAT-00`, `-01`, `-02` … `-99`, plus `PGD-AUDIT-REG-nn` for register-level audits. A
category-keyed identifier is computable without reference to any other session's work and is
therefore collision-free under concurrent authoring. Referred to `RR-002` and to Enterprise
Architecture.

**Significance.** This is the **third independent occurrence** of baseline movement during
authoring — after `AUDIT-016` `AP-17` and `PGD-A1-03` — and the **first to produce an actual
identifier collision rather than a stale figure.** Recommendation `R-02` of `PGD-AUDIT-001` asked
for rule `RG-08` to be ratified after the second occurrence. It was not. This is what the third
occurrence cost.

changed — `PGD-000` is issued and `PG-05` makes identifiers permanent.

**Residual.** The permanent ruling belongs to `RR-002`, which does not exist.

## RQA-29 — 85% of the rule estate is an absolute prohibition

**Severity: High · Type: Design / operating model · Status: OPEN**

| Class | Rules | % |
|:--:|---:|---:|
| `H` hard block | **1,134** | **85.3%** |
| `C` computed | 144 | 10.8% |
| `A` approved override | **39** | **2.9%** |
| `S` soft warning | 9 | 0.7% |
| `C/A`, `C/H` — invalid, two classes | 4 | 0.3% |
| **Total** | **1,330** | 100% |

**Consequence.** Real operations generate exceptions. In a system where 85% of constraints are
absolute and 2.9% have an approval path, staff facing a genuine exception can abandon the
transaction, work around it outside the system, or escalate to someone who also cannot release it.
**The second is what happens**, and it reconstitutes the shadow operation — spreadsheets, phone
calls, WhatsApp — that this ERP exists to eliminate.

**Probable cause, and it is not a design decision.** `H` is the first value in the template's
classification list, the strictest, and the only one requiring no further work: choosing `A`
obliges the author to name an approving authority. **`H` is the path of least authoring
resistance.**

**Remediation.** `RQ-009` `BR-06`/`BR-07` publish a five-test decision for `H` with the burden on
`H`; `BR-27` requires every existing `H` rule to be tested against it; `BR-28` requires the
before/after distribution to be published. Open.
