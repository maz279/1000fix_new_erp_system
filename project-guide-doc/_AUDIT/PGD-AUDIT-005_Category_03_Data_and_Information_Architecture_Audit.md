## 1. Purpose and Method

This audit is **mandatory under the commissioning instruction** and was performed on the nine
Category 03 documents immediately after authoring, before any consumer relied on them.

It applies the five methods `PGD-AUDIT-003` established, plus one added because this category makes
claims about **statute** rather than only about files:

| # | Method | What was checked |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Structural consistency** | Front-matter completeness, house-section conformance, identifier uniqueness, link integrity |
| 2 | **Arithmetic consistency** | Every roll-up figure regenerated from its source rows immediately before issue (`PG-M10`) |
| 3 | **External verification** | Every extraction re-executed against the filesystem; commands recorded |
| 4 | **Temporal verification** | Whether the estate changed during authoring |
| 5 | **Cross-document consistency** | Whether a figure measured in one document is used consistently in the others, and in Category 02 |
| 6 | **Regulatory verification** *(added)* | Statutory claims checked against public sources, with evidentiary standing recorded |

**Result: 18 findings — 3 critical, 6 high, 7 medium, 2 low. Five are closed; thirteen remain open.**

> **One finding is against this authoring session's own work** (`PGD-A5-17`): Category 03 cited two
> fitness functions, `FF-36` and `FF-37`, that did not exist in `AR-012`. **That is precisely the `F3`
> defect class `DA-002` was written to close** — a citation to a register entry that is not there. It
> was caught by method 5 and is closed by extending `AR-012` to 37 checks.

### 1.1 Method 6 and its limits, stated first

Three findings in this category (`PGD-A5-03`, `PGD-A5-08`, and part of `PGD-A5-09`) rest on **public
reporting about Bangladeshi statute, not on the statute itself**.

| # | Rule applied |
|---|---|
| **M6-01** | Every regulatory finding names its sources and is labelled **"requires legal confirmation"** |
| **M6-02** | **No design decision is reversed on a secondary source.** Where the estate's position may be stricter than required, the estate's position is retained and the *question* is corrected |
| **M6-03** | Each is referred to `RR-005` (Standards & Regulatory Currency Register) — the durable control |

> These are **verification findings**, not corrections. The distinction matters: an audit that
> instructed the programme to relax a residency control on a news report would be a worse failure than
> the one it was reporting.

## `PGD-A5-03` — The residency requirement may be a synchronised mirror, not an exclusion

**Severity: Critical · Type: Regulatory verification · Status: OPEN · Evidentiary standing: secondary sources**

**What was found.** The estate designs to **exclusive residency**: classified values live only in a
Bangladesh-resident store and are never duplicated regionally (`05-06` NFR-086, `AR-001` §8.3,
ADR-018, `DA-001` §10).

Public reporting on **Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Ordinance, 2026 (Ordinance No. 23 of 2026),
promulgated 5 February 2026** describes a materially different obligation:

| Dimension | Estate's design | Reported statutory position |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | Value exists **only** in Bangladesh | **At least one synchronised real-time copy** within Bangladesh — a mirror |
| Trigger | Field type — NID, passport, TIN, biometric, recovered data, KYC | **"Restricted personal data"** and Critical Information Infrastructure per the Cyber Security Ordinance 2025 |
| Definition | Enumerated fields | **By impact** — national security, public order, defence, critical infrastructure, fundamental rights |
| Direction | — | The February 2026 amendment **narrowed** the November 2025 scope |

**Why it is Critical.** A mirror and an exclusion are **different schemas**, not different
configurations:

| | Mirror | Exclusion (current design) |
|---|---|---|
| Regional table | Holds the value | Holds a `resident_key uuid` |
| Cross-store join | None | Every read of a classified field |
| Foreign key | Preserved | **Lost** — `DA-001` §6.4 |
| Reconciliation job | None | Nightly, both directions |
| Erasure | One store | Saga across two |

**The estate is currently paying the higher price**, and the second-order costs — lost referential
integrity, a reconciliation job, an orphan class the database used to prevent — are all consequences
of the stricter reading.

**What does not change.** `TR-02`'s position holds: **build the field-level classification mechanism
in Wave 1 regardless**, because classification is data and a change of opinion is a **reclassification,
not a redesign**. Per rule `M6-02`, the audit does not instruct relaxation on a secondary source.

**What must change.** `DEP-31` / `D-13`'s **question**. It currently asks *"does the Authority accept a
resident-key join?"* It should ask: (a) mirror or exclusion; (b) is 1000FiX data within "restricted
personal data" or CII; (c) is field-type classification the right basis, or must it be impact-based?

**Mitigation already built in.** `DA-005` `RD-02` requires an **impact assessment note** on every field
classified `personal` or above, asked at assignment time. That converts `DEP-31`'s answer from a
re-survey of ~17,000 elements into a filter.

**Sources.** [Securiti — PDPA 2026 overview](https://securiti.ai/bangladesh-personal-data-protection-act-overview/) ·
[Digital Policy Alert — Amendment Ordinance No. 23 of 2026](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/38601-president-implemented-personal-data-protection-amendment-ordinance-2026-ordinance-no-23-of-2026) ·
[The Daily Star — *Govt eases data localisation rules*](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/news/govt-eases-data-localisation-rules-drops-jail-terms-tech-firms-4076391).
Referred to `RR-005`.

## `PGD-A5-13` — No mapping exists between the retention schedule and the tables it governs

**Severity: Critical · Type: Missing artefact · Status: OPEN**

**What was found.** `LG-009` assigns retention periods to **data classes** (`RC-01`…`RC-27`) and
**document classes** (`DC-01`…`DC-25`). `DA-001` defines **1,335 tables**. **Nothing joins them.**

`RC-01` reads *"Service order, work order, diagnosis, repair record, evidence photographs"*. That is
prose. The disposal engine must select rows from `fix_service_order`, `fix_work_order`,
`fix_diagnostic_result`, `fix_qc_result` and `fix_service_report` — and no document states that
mapping.

**Why it is Critical.** A required artefact is assumed present when absent. **Without the mapping the
disposal engine cannot select a single row**, which means `LG-009` — 44 KB of correctly researched,
legally grounded retention policy — is **inert**.

It compounds `PGD-A5-11`: even after `M24` and `M60` arrive in W3 and W6, they have nothing to
execute against.

**Remediation.** `DA-006` §3.2 specifies the mapping with five rules, including `RM-02` (a table takes
the longest class of its fields, per `RS-01`) and `RM-03` (an unmapped table cannot be disposed of and
is **reported as a gap** — never silently retained forever nor silently deleted). A worked Category A
mapping is provided. **Populating it across 1,335 tables is `DA-002`'s W0–W9 schedule and has no named
owner** (`DA-006` Q-02).

## `PGD-A5-17` — This session cited two fitness functions before they existed

**Severity: High · Type: Citation to a non-existent register entry · Status: CLOSED at issue**

**What was found.** `DA-002` §7 cites `AR-012` `FF-36` for the citation-reconciliation check;
`DA-009` §5.3 and §9 cite `FF-37` for code-list drift. **`AR-012` v1.0 defined `FF-01`…`FF-35`.
Neither `FF-36` nor `FF-37` existed.**

Six citations across two documents, to two register entries that were not there.

**Why this is worth recording rather than silently fixing.** **`DA-002` exists to close exactly this
defect class.** Its §1 opens with audit `F3`: *"`DT-031` maps nine `MD-028` fields, and none of the
nine exists… A build to `DT-031` would be a build against a schema that does not exist."* Its `RC-2`
check is designed to catch precisely a citation to an unregistered element.

**The document that specifies the check committed the defect the check exists to prevent, in the same
session, six times.**

**Remediation applied.** `AR-012` reissued at **v1.1** with a new §4.6 "Data and metadata" containing
`FF-36` and `FF-37`; the register grows **35 → 37**; all seven count statements updated; both added to
the §10 implementation sequence (`FF-37` at W1, `FF-36` at W2). Verified: 37 defined, 37 in sequence,
zero orphan citations in either direction.

**Root cause and significance.** Category 03 was authored **after** `AR-012` was closed at v1.0. The
natural act — allocating the next free identifier in a sibling register — is safe only if the sibling
register is then updated. **It was not, and nothing checked.** This is the same structural cause as
`PGD-A3-18` (two sessions claiming `PGD-AUDIT-002`): **cross-document identifier allocation with no
allocating authority.** It is the **fourth** occurrence of that cause in this repository, and
`RR-002` remains the unwritten answer.

# 5. Medium and Low Findings

## `PGD-A5-02` — 54 bare `currency` columns

**Severity: Medium · Status: OPEN**

`AS-10` / `DM-07` require money as `Monetary` with an explicit `currency_id` **foreign key**. The
measured specifications declare **`currency` (bare) 54 times and `currency_id` 8 times**.

Unlike the missing `company_id` (12 of 1,370), this is **not omitted boilerplate** — a field *named*
`currency` is a named design choice. A bare text column cannot enforce referential integrity against
`res_currency`, cannot carry rounding precision, and cannot be joined for revaluation.

`DA-001` `M-05` prohibits it; `AR-012` `FF-22` blocks it. **54 specification corrections, cheap now
because no table exists.**

## `PGD-A5-05` — 76% of field names appear in exactly one entity

**Severity: Medium · Status: OPEN**

4,020 of 5,301 distinct field names appear in exactly one entity. Some is genuine domain specificity;
some is 67 authors independently naming the same concept. **It cannot be resolved by inspection** —
`DA-002` §6 specifies a six-step reconciliation clustering by ISO/IEC 11179 `data_element_concept`,
with steward adjudication.

## `PGD-A5-08` — The PDPA penalty basis may be turnover-based

**Severity: Medium · Status: OPEN · Evidentiary standing: secondary sources**

`05-06` §6.3 records exposure as *"up to **BDT 25 lakh**… **BDT 50 lakh** for significant data
fiduciaries"*, registered as risk `RSK-032`. Public reporting describes a **turnover-based** regime:
*"general contraventions attract fines of **1–2% of annual turnover**, while significant data
fiduciaries face **2–5% of annual turnover**."*

The two may coexist. **The estate has recorded only the fixed figures**, and `RSK-032`'s
quantification and the `CO-005` TCO line both rest on them. Referred to Legal and `RR-005`.

## `PGD-A5-09` — One classification axis where three are needed

**Severity: Medium · Status: CLOSED — three axes published**

`05-02` §10's `S0`–`S3` conflates sensitivity, personal-data category and residency. The S3 field
inventory lists **API and integration credentials alongside biometric templates**. A credential has
maximum sensitivity and **no statutory dimension at all** — no data-subject rights, no retention
obligation to a person, no residency constraint.

`DA-005` §3 separates the axes with ten worked examples. **`S0`–`S3` is retained unchanged**, so
nothing already written becomes wrong.

## `PGD-A5-10` — The S3 inventory is nine fields against 5,301 measured

**Severity: Low · Status: CLOSED — recorded**

`05-02` §10's S3 inventory lists nine fields. It is a **worked example, not a classification**, and no
document claims otherwise — but several consume it as though complete. The real artefact is `DA-002`
attribute 21 across ~17,000–19,000 elements, **0% populated**.

## `PGD-A5-12` — The backup erasure position is unconfirmed

**Severity: Medium · Status: OPEN**

`LG-009` `DM-R3`: *"Deletion from the live store does not delete from backup media… **This position
requires G6 confirmation — `O-08`**."* It has not been confirmed.

The backup ladder is **daily 30 days, weekly 1 year, monthly 7 years**. **A record erased today
persists in monthly backups for up to seven years.**

`DA-006` §6.2 supplies the mechanism `DM-R3` asserts: an **erasure ledger** held outside the backup
set (BK-01), **automatic post-restore suppression before return to service** (BK-02), and restore
testing that asserts an erased subject is absent (BK-03). **Without BK-02, `DM-R3` is a hope** — a
restore from a six-month-old backup silently reinstates every erasure in that window.

## `PGD-A5-14` — Two dimensional models exist and neither references the other

**Severity: Medium · Status: CLOSED — reconciled**

`05-02` §9 declares **13 dimensions and 17 facts**; `RP-006` §4.3 declares **18 certified datasets**.
They overlap on 18, diverge on 12, and **contain no cross-reference in either direction**. An engineer
building M62 from `05-02` builds `dim_customer`, `dim_vendor` and `dim_seller`; an analyst reading
`RP-006` expects `ds_party_dim`.

`DA-007` §2.3 reconciles them **as two layers, not two models** — physical beneath semantic — and
merges `dim_customer`/`dim_vendor`/`dim_seller` into one `dim_party`, because three would reproduce in
the warehouse the duplicate-identity problem the OLTP kernel exists to prevent.

## `PGD-A5-16` — `MD-027` `STA-01` is unenforceable as written

**Severity: Medium · Status: CLOSED — denominator derived from schema**

`STA-01` (class **H**): *"Every `fix.*` model with a `state` field must have a complete register entry
set."* **348 models declare a `state`.** `MD-027` §7 estimates 600–1,200 states — an average of 1.7–3.4
per machine, **implausibly low** against `fix.service.order`'s 14.

A hard-block rule cannot fire against an unknown denominator. `DA-009` `ST-01` derives the expected set
from the schema and `FF-37` enforces it.
