## 1. Mandate, Scope and Method

This audit is **mandatory under the commissioning instruction** and was performed immediately after
the eleven Category 00 documents were issued, before any consumer relied on them.

It applies the four methods `PGD-AUDIT-001` established, plus one added because Category 00 makes
quantitative claims about effort, cost and capacity that no prior audit has had to test.

| # | Method | What was checked |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Structural consistency** | Front-matter completeness across 18 fields × 11 files; identifier uniqueness; ID-set agreement between the documents, the category overview and `PGD-000`; cross-document reference resolution |
| 2 | **Arithmetic consistency** | Every roll-up regenerated from its source rows: effort sums, category subtotals, capacity calculations, cost derivations, finding counts, status counts |
| 3 | **External verification** | Every file-count, effort-count, identifier-count and file-absence claim re-executed against the filesystem |
| 4 | **Temporal verification** | Whether the estate changed during authoring — the failure `AUDIT-016` `AP-17` and `PGD-A1-03` both raised |
| 5 | **Source-claim verification** *(added)* | Every quotation attributed to `00-02`, `00-04`, `00-06`, `00-07`, `06-01`, `06-02`, `AUDIT-001` or `PGD-AUDIT-001` traced to its source section and checked word for word |

**Thirteen are closed. Fourteen remain open.**

Of the 27, **eighteen are defects in the existing estate that Category 00's authoring surfaced**,
and **nine are defects in Category 00's own authoring**. The second group is reported at the same
level of detail as the first, because `PGD-A1-13` established that an audit which is soft on its own
author is worth less than no audit.

# 3. Critical Findings

## `PGD-A2-04` — 1,816 requirements are allocated to no wave

**Severity: Critical · Type: Planning gap · Status: OPEN · Owner: Steering Committee**

`06-01` §3 states a requirement count for each of the nine waves. Summed:
142 + 178 + 156 + 189 + 148 + 134 + 121 + 79 + 33 = **1,180**.

That is **exactly** the v1.0 / 30-module requirement total recorded in `H-02`. A fresh enumeration
of distinct `FR-` identifiers across all 67 module files at 2026-08-18 02:06 returns **2,996**.

| Denominator | Source | Requirements with a wave |
|---|---|---:|
| 1,180 | `06-01` §3, summed | 100% |
| ~2,352 | `05-01` title | 50% |
| 2,930 | `00-07` H2-01, 65 files | 40% |
| **2,996** | **This audit, 67 files, 2026-08-18 02:06** | **39%** |

**On the best available denominator, 1,816 requirements — 61% — have no wave.**

**Consequence.** The delivery plan schedules the requirement set of an architecture the programme
abandoned in August 2026. Every wave-level capacity, cost and duration figure derived from `06-01`
inherits that scope.

**Why no prior audit found it.** `C-02` found that `M31` and `M32`–`M67` *"appear in no
register… no wave allocation"* and treated it as a traceability defect. Nobody summed the nine
per-wave requirement figures and compared the total to the estate. It is one line of arithmetic.

**Remediation.** `D-020` places `M31`–`M67`; `RQ-004` settles the denominator; `GV-004` is re-run.
Not closable here — it needs a Steering Committee decision.

## `PGD-A2-05` — The wave plan schedules module identifiers whose meaning changed

**Severity: Critical · Type: Structural contradiction · Status: OPEN · Owner: ARB**

Three module identifiers used by `06-01` mean something different in the 67-module architecture:

| `06-01` schedules | Meant, in the 30-module set | Is, on disk today | The capability moved to |
|---|---|---|---|
| `M20` (W4 core, W7 settlement) | Billing, Revenue Recognition **& Settlement** | `M20_Accounts_Receivable_Invoicing_and_Collections` | **`M53`** Revenue Recognition, Commission & Settlement |
| `M30` (W1 config, W9 multi-entity) | Platform Administration **& Multi-Entity/Multi-Country** | `M30_Platform_Administration_and_Configuration` | **`M65`** Multi-Entity, Multi-Country & Localisation |
| `M12` (W7) | E-Commerce, Parts Retail & Marketplace | `M12_Marketplace_and_Seller_Ecosystem` | E-commerce storefront is **`M38`** |

**The sharpest instance.** Gate 7 criterion `G7-02` requires *"payout accuracy 100% with zero
unexplained variance."* Payout is settlement. Settlement is `M53`. **`M53` is in no wave.** A gate

**Consequence.** Wave 7 and Wave 9 are planned against capabilities that are not where the plan
thinks they are. A vendor building Wave 7 from `06-01` would build against `M20` as it exists —
accounts receivable — and discover at the gate that settlement was never in scope.

**Significance.** This is `C-01` — *"the repository describes two different architectures"* —
reaching the **delivery layer**. `C-01`'s remediation covered `00-01`, `00-02`, `02-01`, `02-02` and
`05-*`. It did not cover `06-01`, and no audit has checked it since.

**Remediation.** `D-019`. Reconcile `06-01` §3 module references to the 67-module register.
Not closable here.

## `PGD-A2-10` — `06-02` §3.2 and §3.4 disagree on three-year benefit by 2.2×–3.3×

**Severity: Critical · Type: Arithmetic inconsistency with financial consequence · Status: OPEN — referred · Owner: CFO**

`06-02` §3.2 is headed *"Cumulative Benefit (Base, BDT lac)"* and lists **45 · 310 · 645 · 876 ·
920** for Years 1–5.

**The header and the values contradict each other.** If cumulative, Year 4 could not equal the
§3.1 annual run rate of 876, and Year 5 could not rise by only 44. The row notes settle it —
*"Year 4 (steady state) 876 — full run rate"* — so the values are **annual**, mislabelled.

Under either reading, `06-02` §3.4's base-case three-year benefit of **$1.96M** is unsupported by
§3.2 in the same document:

| Reading | 3-year benefit | vs §3.4's $1.96M |
|---|---:|---:|
| Annual (what the notes say): 45 + 310 + 645 = 1,000 lac | ~$909K | **2.2× overstated** |
| Cumulative (what the header says): 645 lac at Year 3 | ~$586K | **3.3× overstated** |

**Consequence, and why it is Critical.** `06-02` §3.4 is the ROI table: *"Base case — 3-Yr Benefit
$1.96M, 3-Yr Cost $610K, ROI 221%, Payback 17 months."* **On §3.2's own curve, and at the approved
cost, base-case payback is roughly 27 months, not 17.** This is independent of the cost
restatement in `GV-008` — it is an error on the *benefit* side, which three audits have described
as the sound side of the model.

**Why no prior audit found it.** `00-06` §7 checked that *"benefit figures reconcile between
`00-02`, `06-02` and the module files"* — and they do, at the §3.1 annual level. Nobody
cross-checked §3.1 against §3.2 against §3.4 inside the same document.

**Remediation.** Correct the §3.2 column header; restate §3.4 from §3.2's curve. `BM-003`.
**Referred to the CFO — `06-02` is outside this repository's authority under `PG-M8`.**

## `PGD-A2-15` — 644 files. Zero under version control

**Severity: Critical · Type: Configuration management · Status: OPEN · Owner: CTO**

Verified directly at 2026-08-18 02:06: **no `.git`, `.hg` or `.svn` directory exists at any level
of the workspace.** All 644 files across five repositories are unversioned.

| Unavailable | Consequence |
|---|---|
| History, diff, attribution | *"What changed, when, by whom"* is unanswerable for every file |
| Branch, merge | Concurrent authoring produces the baseline movement recorded three times (§8) |
| Rollback | A bad edit is permanent |
| Automated pre-commit checks | `PG-M9` links, `PG-M10` roll-ups, `PG-M11` timestamps are **all manual, and all three have failed** |

**Significance — this is a root cause, not a finding among findings.** Of the five recurring
defect classes in `GV-010` §6.4, **four would be caught mechanically by a pre-commit hook**: stale
roll-ups, registers denying work that exists, baseline movement during authoring, and broken links.
That is 18 of the estate's recorded instances.

**Why no prior audit found it.** Four audits examined the *contents* of files. An audit that reads
files cannot see the absence of the mechanism that would let it read *changes*.

**Remediation.** `GV-011` §10.2 — six steps, the first three deliverable in two weeks.
Steps 4 and 5 alone would have prevented **twenty broken references and seven arithmetic
findings, two of them critical.**

**Severity: Critical · Type: Identifier-space violation · Status: OPEN — referred · Owner: Enterprise Architecture**
