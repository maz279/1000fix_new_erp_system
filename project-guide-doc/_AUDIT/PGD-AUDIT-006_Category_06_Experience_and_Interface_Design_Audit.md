## 1. Purpose and Method

This audit is **mandatory under the commissioning instruction** and was performed immediately after
the eight Category 06 documents were issued, before any consumer relied on them.

It applies the four methods established by `PGD-AUDIT-001`:

| # | Method | What was checked |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Structural consistency** | Front-matter completeness against `_templates/TPL_Guiding_Document.md`; mandatory section set; identifier uniqueness across **all five repositories**; internal identifier uniqueness **within the category**; link and path resolution |
| 2 | **Arithmetic consistency** | Every roll-up regenerated by counting its source rows immediately before issue (`PG-M10`) |
| 3 | **External verification** | Every count, keyword and **absence** claim re-executed against the filesystem |
| 4 | **Temporal verification** | Whether the estate changed between first enumeration and issue (`PG-M11`) |

**Result: 6 critical, 10 high, 9 medium and 1 low finding — 26 in total.
Nineteen are closed. Seven remain open.**

**Seven of the twenty-six are against Category 06's own work.** Two overstated absence claims
(`UXA-16`, `UXA-20`), one roll-up that did not match its rows (`UXA-22`), one register that omitted
a row and mis-derived a subtotal (`UXA-23`), four identifier collisions (`UXA-24`), a stale estate
figure (`UXA-25`), and an audit-identifier near-collision this audit avoided only by checking
(`UXA-26`). **All seven were found and corrected before issue.**

Each is recorded rather than smoothed over, on the principle `PGD-AUDIT-001` established and
`PGD-AUDIT-004` repeated: *a category whose own error history is hidden cannot ask the rest of the
programme to disclose theirs.*

> ### The finding this audit most wants read
>
> **`UXA-16` and `UXA-20` are the same defect: an absence asserted from an incomplete search.**
> That is `RQA-33`, recorded by `PGD-AUDIT-004` against Category 01 eighteen hours earlier, occurring
> **twice more** in the very next category. An absence claim is the most persuasive and least
> reliable sentence a document can contain, and this estate now has three recorded instances.
> **Recommendation `R-04` makes the sweep mandatory and auditable.**

# 3. Critical Findings

## `UXA-01` — `NFR-080` cannot be satisfied as written

**Severity: Critical · Type: Requirement feasibility · Status: CLOSED by ruling**

`NFR-080` and `PS-06` require *"a consistent design system across all 12 portals — **one component
library, one token set**."* Counted from the Technology Stack Specification §11 at
2026-08-18 14:45 (+06), the twelve surfaces run on **five distinct rendering runtimes**: Odoo OWL 2,
Odoo Portal QWeb, Next.js/React 19, Flutter/Dart, and embedded Metabase + Grafana.

**Dart cannot consume a CSS custom property. QWeb cannot consume a React component.** One component
*library* — one package of one implementation — is not achievable in any combination of these.

**Consequence had it not been found.** The requirement would have been marked satisfied in Wave 1
on the strength of a shared token file, and the twelve surfaces would have diverged in component
behaviour with nothing detecting it — which is precisely the outcome `NFR-080` exists to prevent.

**Closure.** `UX-001` §3.2 rules the satisfiable form — *one token source, one component contract,
five realisations, one conformance suite* — and supplies the compensating controls `CFM-04`
(inventory parity) and `CFM-05` (visual parity across runtimes, nightly). The restatement is raised
to `GV-003` as `UX-001-D-01`. **Until the Change Board rules, both statements are live and the
stricter reading is built to.**

## `UXA-10` — the conflict disclosure contradicts the architecture

**Severity: Critical · Type: Cross-document contradiction · Status: CLOSED by ruling**

`UI-009` §3.3 provides the copy keys `common.conflict.keep_mine` (*"Keep my version"*) and
`common.conflict.keep_theirs`. `AR-010` §5.3 specifies a **server-authoritative authority matrix**:
assignment, pricing, stock, order state and entitlement are decided by the server; evidence, notes,
field timestamps and measurements are retained from the client. `AR-010` §5.4 lists four outcomes —
Applied, Applied with conflict, Rejected, Deferred — **and none of them is a user choice.**

**Consequence had it not been found.** A technician offline for four hours, whose job was reassigned
at 10:55, would have been offered *"Keep my version"* for an assignment the server had already
changed — a choice the system would then overrule. **That is worse than offering no choice**, because
the entire value of the offline design rests on the technician trusting the sync indicator
(`AR-010` §6.1). One overrulable choice destroys that trust permanently.

**Closure.** `UX-004` §5.8 and `UX-006` §8.3 rule that `AR-010` governs; the two keys are
**withdrawn**; `common.conflict.manual` (deferral to a supervisor) is confirmed as already correct.
`UX-006` §8.2 specifies the five-element disclosure layout and §8.4 gives two worked cases.
`FV-06` asserts the absence of the withdrawn strings at build time. Replacement copy — approximately
**nine new keys** across conflict outcomes, > 24 h offline and storage pressure — is raised to
`UI-009` under `CG-006` as `UX-006` `Q-05`.

## `UXA-11` — the WCAG ruling has not propagated

**Severity: Critical · Type: Currency · Status: OPEN**

`RQ-006` §3.7 ruled **WCAG 2.2 AA** governs on 2026-08-18. Executed the same day at 14:45 (+06)
across the 621 demand-side files:

```
grep -rli "WCAG 2.1" 1000fix_ERP_Module primary-doc project_content old-primary-doc | wc -l   → 100
grep -rli "WCAG 2.2" 1000fix_ERP_Module primary-doc project_content old-primary-doc | wc -l   →   4
```

**One hundred files still state 2.1** — including `PS-04`, `NFR-073` in `05-06`, `CG-011`,
**`TS-016` itself**, all twelve portal specifications and the surface-summary block of every `UI-*`
copy pack.

**Consequence.** `TS-016` will test the 2.1 criteria set. **The four criteria new at AA in 2.2 —
2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured, 2.5.7 Dragging Movements, 2.5.8 Target Size, 3.3.8 Accessible
Authentication — would not be tested**, and 2.5.7 is the one the estate already knows it fails
(`U-09`). A surface could pass its accessibility gate while failing the criterion the programme
identified as its material gap.

**Not closed.** This is a **citation update, not a redesign** — 2.2 is a superset of 2.1, so nothing
built is invalidated. It must be raised as **one coordinated change** under `CG-006` and `GV-003`,
not as 100 independent edits. Priority W1. Owner unassigned (`UX-005` `Q-02`).

## `UXA-12` — the most exposed surfaces were outside accessibility scope

**Severity: Critical · Type: Scope · Status: CLOSED by ruling**

`PS-04` and `NFR-073` say *"WCAG 2.1 AA compliance on all **web portals**."* `TS-016` §1.3 reads
that literally, scopes **nine** surfaces, and marks `P02`, `P09` and `P11` as *"Not covered by
`PS-04`"*.

Those three are the **technician**, **rider** and **field auditor** applications. `URD` §21.3
identifies their users as carrying the most severe situational impairments in the programme:

| Persona | Condition | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| `A-02` Kabir | One hand occupied, direct sunlight, gloves, noise | **Always** |
| `A-03` Jasim | Helmet, gloves, motorcycle, rain | Daily |
| `A-04` Rubel | Low literacy, Bengali only, both hands full | Structural |

**A requirement phrased "web portals" has excluded four applications from an obligation that
applies to them more strongly than to any portal.**

**Closure.** `UX-005` `ACD-01` rules **all thirteen surfaces in scope**, with the four Flutter
applications conforming to WCAG 2.2 AA **as interpreted by WCAG2ICT** (W3C Group Note, WCAG 2.2
edition, 11 December 2025). `ACD-02`…`ACD-05` state what that changes, and `ACD-05` requires
`TS-016` §1.3 to be re-scoped with **three new script sets**. Raised as `UX-005` `Q-05`.

## `UXA-19` — baselines that become unmeasurable at cutover

**Severity: Critical · Type: Benefits assurance · Status: OPEN**

`04-00` states several KPI baselines as **"unmeasured"** while stating targets against them:

| KPI | Baseline | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Booking funnel conversion (`P01`) | **unmeasured** | > 72% |
| Self-service status checks vs hotline | ~0 | > 80% of enquiries |
| Portal monthly active users | — | > 120,000 |
| App store rating | — | > 4.5 |

**Not closed.** `UX-008` `UTP-39`…`UTP-41` require a benchmark evaluation before each surface's
first release, and `UTP-40` flags the pre-cutover legacy measurements as a `MG-*` / `BM-001`
