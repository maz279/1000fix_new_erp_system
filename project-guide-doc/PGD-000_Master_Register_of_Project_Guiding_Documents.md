### 0.2 The single most important finding

> **A vendor cannot build from the current estate.** The module specifications are written at
> *requirement* altitude — `FR-SRM-014: "The system shall…"` — which is correct and valuable, but a
> requirement is not a design. Between "the system shall reserve a spare part against a job card"
> and a developer writing `cd_service_order/models/service_order.py` there are four missing
> artefacts: the **SRS** (testable, consolidated, traced), the **SAD** (structure, components,
> runtime, deployment views), the **SDD** (module-level design, classes, algorithms, schema) and
> the **API/Event contracts** (what is actually called).
>
> The programme has 2,930+ requirements and **zero** design specifications.

### 0.3 Status legend and evidence standard

A status is an **assertion about the filesystem**, and every assertion needs a test. The
"evidence required" column is that test. A status may not be set or changed without it —
rule `PG-M2`, and audit finding `PGD-A1-08`.

| Symbol | Status | Meaning | **Evidence required to assign it** |
|:--:|---|---|---|
| ✅ | **Exists** | A controlled file on disk discharges this document's purpose | **All four:** (a) a named file exists at a stated path; (b) it carries complete front matter per `RR-003`; (c) it covers ≥90% of the section set its governing standard requires, counted section by section; (d) it has a named owner and an approval record |
| ◐ | **Partial** | Useful source material exists, but no controlled artefact does | **At least one** named file and section is cited in the register row. **Read ◐ as "absent, with useful source material" — not as "nearly done."** ◐ never becomes ✅ by assertion; it requires the four ✅ tests |
| ⬜ | **Missing** | Nothing on disk | `test -f` returns false for every candidate path, and no cited source covers it |
| ⛔ | **Missing — blocking** | As ⬜, **and** a dependency already rests on it | As ⬜, **plus** a named Wave 1 activity, statutory obligation, go-live gate or open audit finding that cannot proceed without it. The dependency must be named in the row |
| ⚠ | **Exists — defective** | On disk, but a named audit finding has invalidated part of it | A ✅ document **plus** a cited open finding. **⚠ is a subset of ✅, never counted separately** |
| ➖ | **Not applicable yet** | Legitimately deferred | A recorded decision in `GV-003` naming the deferral and its review date. **Currently used by zero documents** — reserved so that a future deferral is a decision, not an omission |

> **The ◐ population is the one to watch.** **Four** of 140 documents — **3%** — carry ◐, down from 38 at v1.2 and from 6 at v1.9. That is the
> status most likely to be misread as progress, and it covers the largest population. Until
> `RR-003` publishes the per-document-type section sets that the ✅ test (c) measures against, no
> ◐ may be promoted to ✅.
