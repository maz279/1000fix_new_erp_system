## 1. Purpose

`CM-008` defines what a champion is, how they are selected, what they may and may not do, how they
escalate, and how the network is measured. `TR-023` trains them. Both are good files, and
`CM-008` §4.2 already closed the `AUDIT-009` `TR-08` duplication finding with a twelve-row
ownership table.

**Neither addresses the binding constraint, and both say so.**

> `CM-008` `O-01` raises it. `TR-023` `O-02` identifies it as *"the central risk to the delivery
> model."* `06-01` §4 states it as a fact and calls the business-side commitment table **"the
> binding constraint on the programme."** `GV-005` §4.2 records `RSK-001` — super-user availability
> — as **Critical and ⬜ Untreated**, with decision `D-002` open since **2026-08-12**.
>
> **The constraint is that champions and super-users have day jobs in a business serving 250,000
> customers a month, and nobody has released them.**

A charter is the instrument that releases named individuals for a stated proportion of their time,
with their line manager's agreement, a backfill decision and a cost. **Without it the network
exists on paper and not in the branches**, and every mechanism that depends on it — training
delivery at 51+ sites, floor-walking through hypercare, UAT acceptance, local adoption — inherits
its absence.

## 3. The Two Populations

### 3.1 They are different people doing different work

`CM-008` `NW-01` governs and is not restated. The **charter-relevant** difference:

| | **Branch champion** | **Module super-user** |
|---|---|---|
| Population | **51+** — one per branch | **40–60** — 2–3 per module |
| Belongs to | A **site** | A **module** |
| **Committed time (`06-01` §4)** | **4 hours/week, W1–W5** | **30% during UAT, 10% otherwise** |
| Primary job | Local adoption | **Requirement acceptance at UAT** |
| Decision right | Local training sequencing; cutover timing at their site | **Whether a requirement is met** — and `G1-11` makes their signature a gate criterion |
| Risk if absent | `RSK-008`, `RSK-009` | **`RSK-001` — the highest-rated risk in the programme** |
| Released by | Their branch manager | Their department head |
| Backfilled by | §5.3 | §5.3 |

> **`CNC-02` — neither is a job title; both are assignments carried by people whose registered role
> is in `05-04`** (`NW-02`). Creating them as roles would require an `MD-009` change and an access
> profile, and would turn an adoption mechanism into an establishment question. **The release,
> however, is an establishment question, and §5 treats it as one.**

### 3.2 Two commitments, one person

**`CNC-03`: a person may hold one network assignment, not two.** At 51+ sites and 17 module owners
the temptation to appoint the same capable branch person as both champion and super-user is
strong, and it produces a person committed for `4 h/week + 30%` in the same wave, which is not a
part-time assignment.

Where the same individual is genuinely the only candidate for both, the **union** of the two
commitments is stated in the release letter and backfilled accordingly — never the larger of the
two. This mirrors `CU-08`'s treatment of dual-role curricula.

### 3.3 Where the programme's model departs from Kotter, and why it matters

Kotter's network is **volunteer**: people opt in, and the energy of the network comes from the
opting in. The 1000FiX model is **nominated**: `CM-008` §4.3 has the branch manager nominate and
the Change Lead confirm.

| | Volunteer | **Nominated (this programme)** |
|---|---|---|
| Source of energy | Self-selection | **The release, and the recognition** |
| Failure mode | Enthusiasts without credibility | **Appointed people nobody asks** — `NW-05`'s exact warning |
| Required compensation | — | **A real release, a real deputy, and a recognition that counts** (§5, §6, §7) |

> **`CNC-04` — because this network is nominated rather than volunteer, the release and the
> recognition are not benefits; they are the mechanism.** A volunteer network survives an unhonoured
> promise because the volunteers wanted the role. A nominated network does not.

### 5.3 Backfill — the decision nobody has taken

**`CNC-08`: released time is backfilled, absorbed, or the release is not real. There is no fourth
option, and "absorbed" is a decision with a named consequence, not a default.**

| Option | What it means | Consequence | Cost |
|:--:|---|---|---|
| **A — Backfill** | Temporary or additional headcount covers the released hours | The network runs as designed | 1,190–1,622 PW of cover at branch and functional rates |
| **B — Absorb** | The person does both jobs | **The network decays.** `TR-023` `O-02` and `CM-008` `O-01` both predict this; `NW-12`'s turnover threshold (< 15% per wave) is the detector | Nil on the budget line; paid in adoption failure and attrition |
| **C — Reduce the ask** | Fewer champions, or fewer hours | Must be a decision with the coverage consequence stated. **`CNC-14` makes 100% site coverage a cutover precondition**, so reducing champion count reduces the sites that can go live | Nil |
| **D — Do nothing** | The current position | `RSK-001` materialises. **`GV-005` records it as Critical and untreated with `D-002` open since 2026-08-12** | Programme |

> **`CNC-09` — the programme is currently on option D, and option D is not on the list of
> acceptable outcomes.** This charter's function is to force A, B or C to be chosen and recorded in
> `GV-003`. `CNC-Q-02`.

### 5.4 The release letter

**`CNC-10`: the release is a letter, signed by the line manager and countersigned by the CEO for
super-users, stating eight things.** A verbal agreement in a corridor is the mechanism that has
failed in every programme that recorded `RSK-001`.

| # | The letter states |
|:--:|---|
| 1 | The **named individual** and their registered `05-04` role |
| 2 | Which assignment — champion or super-user — and for which site or module |
| 3 | **The hours per week, and the period, in dates anchored to the wave gate** (`CA-02`) |
| 4 | **What work will not be done, or who will do it** — the backfill decision under §5.3 |
| 5 | The named **deputy** |
| 6 | What the assignment counts for in the appraisal — §7.2 |
| 7 | The escalation route if the release is not honoured — §8 |
| 8 | The end date, and the handover at it |

> **Item 4 is the one that makes the letter real.** A release that does not say what stops being
> done is a statement of intent. `06-01` §4's own words — *"if business availability is not
> protected, waves will slip regardless of development capacity"* — describe the outcome when item
> 4 is left blank.

## 7. Continuity, Recognition and What It Counts For

### 7.1 Deputies and continuity

**`CNC-12`: every site names a deputy** (`NW-07`). One person per site, across 51+ sites, five waves
and thirty-six months, guarantees absence at go-live somewhere.

**`CNC-13`: the deputy is trained, not merely named.** `TR-023` is 16 hours; a deputy who has not
taken it is a name on a card. This doubles the champion side of the `TR-023` population from
**51–60 to 102–120**, and takes the whole `TR-023` population — champions, deputies and
super-users — to **142–180 people and 2,272–2,880 learner-hours**. `CH-002` §4.4 and §6.3 are
computed on that basis.

**`CNC-14`: 100% of sites having a confirmed, published, trained champion **and** deputy is a
cutover precondition** (`NW-16`, `CH-001` `PR-1`). A site below it is not ready, and the readiness
board reports it as such.

### 7.2 The appraisal decision

`CM-008` §4.8 records recognition as *"specific, public and non-monetary by default"*, and adds one
line that is a decision rather than a practice:

> *"Reference in the appraisal — **requires HR agreement** — the assignment must count for something
> in the system that decides progression, or it will not be sustained."*

**`CNC-15`: HR decides, explicitly, whether the network assignment counts in the appraisal, and the
decision is recorded either way.** An unanswered request is answered by default in the negative,
and the population finds out at their appraisal.

| Element | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Owner | **HR Director** |
| Needed by | **W1** — before the first cohort is confirmed |
| Options | Counts as a stated objective · counts as a development activity · does not count |
| **If it does not count** | That is a legitimate answer, and it is **stated in the release letter** so nobody discovers it later |
| Recorded in | `GV-003` |

### 7.3 The recognition boundary

`CM-008` §4.8 and `CM-017` `RN-08` govern. **`CNC-16`: champions are never ranked against each
other.** `M57` `BRU-PIC-16` is a Class `H` block on peer comparison with no waiver route, and
[`CH-004`](CH-004_Adoption_Measurement_and_Reinforcement_Plan.md) §7.3 carries the four permitted

## 8. When the Release Is Not Honoured

**`CNC-18`: a breach of the release is a programme escalation, not a local scheduling matter.**

| Situation | Detection | Escalates to | Within |
|---|---|---|---|
| A champion misses two consecutive forums | `NW-17` attendance record | **The branch manager**, not the champion | Next forum |
| A site's champion has not been released in practice for two consecutive weeks | Forum attendance + question flow at zero (`NW-12`) | Operations Director | Weekly readiness board |
| **A super-user falls below 25% availability in a UAT month** | UAT session attendance | **CEO** — this is `RSK-001`'s registered trigger and the CEO is its named owner | **Immediately** (`CM-002` §4.8) |
| A site has no champion or no deputy at T−1 week | §6 register | Operations Director; **the site is `Not ready`** | 48 hours |
| Network turnover above 15% in a wave | `NW-12` | **Steering Committee** — the role is unsustainable as defined | At the gate |
| A champion is asked to act outside `CM-008` §4.4 — typically to grant access | Champion escalation | Branch manager **and** Change Lead | **Immediately — a control matter** (`NW-08`, `NW-18`) |

> **`CNC-19` — the `RSK-001` trigger is the only one in the RAID register whose escalation route is
> the CEO, and it has never fired because nothing measures it.** `GV-005` states the trigger —
> *"any super-user below 25% availability in a UAT month"* — and no instrument produces that
> figure. §6's register and the UAT attendance record are that instrument. **Standing it up is the
> first act of treating `RSK-001`.**
