## Purpose

`project_content` categories **08 `TR`** (24 files) and **16 `CM`** (18 files) hold 42 files of
training and communication **content** — curricula, 15 role packs, question banks, job aids,
town-hall scripts, transition kits, adoption campaigns.

What was missing was the **plan** that resources, schedules and measures it.
`06-05_Change_Management_and_Training_Plan.md` was planned and never written. **It is now
superseded, not awaited**: `CH-001` is its change half and `CH-002` its training half.
