## 2. What was built

**High-Value Cash ID Check** (`fix.cash.id.check`, cd_front_desk 19.0.2.1.0, `models/fix_m04_cash_id.py`):
- A cash counter sale above the threshold is refused at confirm until its check exists. The threshold is M51 statutory master data read through M20 (`fix.ar.receipt._nid_threshold`, BDT 50,000 today).
- Recording the check: (1) the customer's consent is created against the transparency-notice version shown, **before** (2) the NID is written to the Bangladesh-resident store as an M33 identity document, or an NID already on file is used. A PDPA processing record (lawful basis: legal obligation) is written.
- The check keeps only the last 4 digits. It cannot be changed or deleted. Only a cashier or branch manager records one.
- No NID is taken when it is not needed: below the threshold, or not cash.
- The rule is in `cd_front_desk` because `cd_front_desk_finance` is installed on fix_real only (not on fix_dev or the test template). `cd_front_desk_finance` 19.0.0.2.0 extends it to the basket: one check covers the basket's one cash payment.

**Shift Handover** (`fix.shift.handover`, `models/fix_m04_capture.py`):
- The outgoing shift writes notes and open items to a named incoming team member, who acknowledges them.
- The author cannot acknowledge their own handover; a branch manager signs for an incoming member who has no login.
- Once acknowledged, a handover is frozen. It is never deleted.
- Page 8's "+ New" now opens it. Cash shifts are still opened from page 6 and from the chip.

**Customer Rating at Handover** (`fix.service.order.counter_rating`, `counter_feedback`):
- Taken once, on a ready or released order, with who took it and when stamped.
- Page 4 chip "Customer Rating at Handover" uses a pick-the-order form.

**Page 9** now lists the deposits recorded (`M04-deposits`, M50's `fix.cash.deposit`) next to the variance report.
