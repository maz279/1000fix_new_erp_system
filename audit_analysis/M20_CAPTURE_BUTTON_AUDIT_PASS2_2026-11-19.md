## 2. What was built

`cd_billing_engine` 19.0.2.5.0, `models/fix_ar_capture.py` (imported last), `data/fix_ar_capture_data.xml` (8 sequences, 4 daily jobs), ACLs, and isolation rules regenerated by `tooling/generators/generate-isolation-rules.py`.

| Page | Record | Rules it enforces |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | **Invoice Delivery** (+ New) | Issued invoices only. One record per invoice and channel: sending again is a Retry on the same record, up to 3 retries (`cd_billing_engine.delivery_max_retries`), then another channel. The recipient is checked: an email address, or a mobile number of 10+ digits. It carries the invoice's existing payment link, so no second link is made (T-12). Status changes only through Sent → Delivered → Opened / Failed. |
| 4 | **Customer Statement** (chip) | Generate takes a snapshot of `compute_customer_statement`: opening, charges, credits, closing and the lines. The statement is then frozen and is not regenerated. Mark Delivered names a channel. |
| 5 | **Deposit / Retention** (chip) | Draws on a confirmed receipt's unapplied money. A held deposit is no longer "unapplied", so it is not escalated. Apply posts an allocation to a named invoice. Release raises a refund; rejecting that refund puts the deposit back on hold. |
| 8 | **Dunning Policy** (chip) | A ladder per segment (retail / corporate / all) or per named account. Each of the 3 levels has its days, channel and tone; L3 names who it escalates to. It is activated by a second Accounting Administrator. An active ladder is frozen (retire it and replace it), and only one is active per segment. **The dunning run now reads it.** The `LADDER` constants are only the fallback. Order of choice: the named account's ladder, then the segment's, then "all", then the constants. |
| 8 | **Promise to Pay** (chip) | Against an open issued invoice, for at most what is still open, dated from today to 30 days ahead (`promise_max_days`). One open promise per invoice. Dunning is held: the run records the suppression (who, why, until) and no new reminder is sent. The daily check marks the promise kept when the money arrives. Otherwise, after the date it is broken: dunning resumes and the customer's collection case escalates (AUT-ARB-12). |
| 8 | **Payment Plan** (chip) | Instalments in a grid. A second Accountant agrees the plan. The instalments must add up to what is open on the plan's invoices. While the plan runs, dunning on those invoices is held. A missed instalment defaults it (tolerance `payment_plan_max_missed`, default 0): dunning resumes and a case opens. The instalments are frozen once agreed. |
| 8 | **Collection Case** (chip) | One open case per customer. It is opened automatically by an L3 escalation, a broken promise or a defaulted plan, or by a collector. Priority = overdue BDT thousands + oldest overdue days + 50 per broken promise; the worklist is sorted by it. Actions: Log Contact, Take, Refresh. It closes with an outcome; "paid" is refused while money is still overdue. |
| 9 | **Refund** (chip; it used to open the credit note) | For money on account: a receipt's unapplied part, or a released deposit. Allocated money is returned through a credit note. Another person authorises it: an Accountant up to BDT 10,000 (`refund_direct_max`), an Accounting Administrator above that. Cash above BDT 10,000 is refused (BRU-ARB-11). Authorisation raises a pending M50 instruction (`fix.payment.execution`); bKash, Nagad and Rocket refunds go out on `mobile_fs`. |
| 10 | **Unbilled Exception** (+ New) | The daily job turns each closed service order with no invoice into an exception. It closes the exception itself once an invoice appears, and ages every open one: branch manager at 1 day, Finance Manager at 3, CFO at 7 (`unbilled_escalation_days`, BRU-ARB-24). A person reports by hand what the job does not read: contract, rental, marketplace, subscription and managed-service streams. "Not billable" is approved by an Accounting Administrator who did not report it (BRU-ARB-01). "Invoiced" must name an issued invoice of the same customer. |

Receipts: `unapplied = received − allocated − held deposits − refunds`. The allocation total check now also refuses over-committing a receipt.

## 4. Open items (owner)

- **No ladder is active yet.** Until an Accounting Administrator writes one and another activates it, dunning keeps the built-in NT-006 ladder.
- **Delivery records confirmation; it does not send.** The email, WhatsApp and SMS channels are still in the porting phase, so a person records Sent / Delivered.
- **The reconciliation job reads service orders only.** The other revenue streams are reported by hand until their modules raise billable events.
- **No suppression for page 1's failed events.** BRU-ARB-01 "approved suppression" is covered for unbilled work, but not for failed billable events on page 1. That needs a decision on who approves it.
- **The segment comes from the customer's company flag** (corporate vs individual). M33 has no AR segment field. Brand partners and strategic accounts use named-account ladders.
