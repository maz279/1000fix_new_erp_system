  returned Protection Off / Fully Decrypted**. An elevated re-run of the
  verifier therefore reports T03 as **FAIL**, and per `DE-12`/`SC-009`
  (encryption non-negotiable) and `DE-19` (no work begins on a red
  baseline) **the workstation baseline is RED until the operator enables
  BitLocker** (`manage-bde -on C:`, recovery key saved first — deliberately
  never done silently by tooling; boot-lockout risk).

  `wslrelay`/`com.docker.backend`) publish 5432/6379/9000 at the time of
  the run. No **host** service holds them (the `DE-01` violation class is
  absent); it is a port-collision risk for the future 1000fix-erp stack —
  remedy in the evidence JSON (stop or remap before the stack binds them).
