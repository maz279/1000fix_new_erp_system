## 1. Executive Board Resolution

> **BOARD RESOLUTION BM-2026-10-D002:**
> "The Board of Directors reviews the operational resourcing models for User Acceptance Testing (UAT) across the 67 ERP modules. Acknowledging that an unphased 134–201 super-user commitment across all modules simultaneously would disrupt daily branch operations, the Board hereby approves **Option C (Phased Commitment)**.
>
> 1. The 18 critical Priority-0 (P0) modules shall be allocated 2 to 3 dedicated, experienced branch managers, lead service engineers, and senior finance officers (total 36 super-users).
> 2. The 49 Priority-1 and Priority-2 (P1/P2) modules shall each be allocated 1 designated lead super-user supported by role-based testing pools (total 49 super-users).
> 3. All 85 named individuals are formally relieved of 30% of their routine operational duties during active wave UAT cycles, with their operational duties backfilled by junior staff."
