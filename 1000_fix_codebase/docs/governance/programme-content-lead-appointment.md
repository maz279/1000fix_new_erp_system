## 1. Executive Resolution

The Programme Steering Committee hereby formally appoints the **Programme Content Lead** (`AP-01`), effective immediately.

This appointment fulfills the mandatory governance condition recorded in `CG-001 §0` and `RF-010 AP-01`, and formally closes Risk `CR-01` and Risk `CR-03` recorded in `RF-011 Content Risk Register`.

---

## 2. Terms of Appointment & Sign-off Authority

In accordance with `CG-002 Content Operating Model and RACI` §5:

1. **Class E (Internal Governance) Authority:** The Programme Content Lead holds sole Accountable (`A`) sign-off authority for all 85 Class E content assets, including the 12 Reference Registers (`RF-001` through `RF-012`) and 14 Content Governance policies (`CG-001` through `CG-014`).
2. **Class C (Operational-Critical) & Class D (User-Facing) Co-Authority:** The Programme Content Lead holds co-signoff authority alongside Domain Content Owners and the Business Owners Forum.
3. **Statutory G6 Gateway Conformance:** The Programme Content Lead is empowered to formally certify the statutory accuracy and operational readiness of all 452 content files in the `project_content` master index, working alongside Legal Counsel and the Chief Financial Officer for the 32 statutory Class A and B files.
