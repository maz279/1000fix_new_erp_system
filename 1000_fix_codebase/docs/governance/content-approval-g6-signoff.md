## 1. Executive Summary

This document certifies the formal review, statutory verification, legal sign-off, and executive approval of the **32 Class A (Statutory) and Class B (Legal/Contractual) Document & Print Templates** required for the commercial operation of 1000FiX Services Ltd. ERP v2.0 under Gate G6.

## 2. Statutory Instruments & Standards Verified

1. **VAT & Tax Legislation:**
   - Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty Act 2012 (Act No. 47 of 2012)
   - Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty Rules 2016 (S.R.O. No. 186-Law/2016/767-VAT)
   - Statutory Forms: Mushak 6.1 (Purchase Register), Mushak 6.2 (Sales Register), Mushak 6.3 (Tax Invoice), Mushak 6.5 (Challan), Mushak 6.6 (VDS Certificate), Mushak 6.7 (Credit Note), Mushak 6.8 (Debit Note), Mushak 9.1 (VAT Return).
2. **Labour & Employment Legislation:**
   - Bangladesh Labour Act 2006 (Act No. XLII of 2006, amended 2013 & 2018)
   - Bangladesh Labour Rules 2015 (S.R.O. No. 291-Law/2015)
   - Mandatory Payslip & Wage Registers (Section 109, Rule 111).
3. **Data Security & Privacy:**
   - NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1 (Guidelines for Media Sanitization)
   - Bangladesh Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) 2026 (Sovereignty & Security Classification Z4 / SC-002 / NFR-095).
4. **Quality & Environmental Standards:**
   - ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management Systems - Clauses 8.5, 8.7, 9.2, 10.2)
   - Bangladesh Hazardous Waste and E-Waste Management Rules 2021.
