Dear Counsel,

1000FiX Services Limited ("the Company") hereby formally engages your firm to provide an authoritative, written legal opinion on five specific statutory questions arising from the **Personal Data Protection Act 2026 (Act No. XII of 2026)**, the **Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Ordinance No. 23 of 2026 (promulgated 5 February 2026)**, and any related rules and regulatory orders issued by the Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh.

This opinion is strictly required by the Board of Directors and the Architecture Review Board to settle Architecture Decision **`D-013`** concerning the data residency architecture of the Company's nationwide ERP and after-sales service platform.

---

## 2. Scope of Mandate: The Five Questions

Counsel is instructed to answer, in writing, citing the specific statutory provisions, sections, and authoritative administrative rulings, the following five questions:

| Question ID | Question Counsel Must Answer in Writing |
|:--:|---|
| **Q1** | **Restricted Personal Data Classification:** Which of the Company's customer, device, technician, and transaction fields fall within the Act's statutory definition of **"restricted personal data"** under Section 3(42) and Section 28 — field-by-field, against the 22-field candidate list annexed hereto as **Annexure A**? |
| **Q2** | **Residency Mechanism (Replica vs Exclusive Storage):** Does the statutory obligation under Section 28 (as amended by Amendment Ordinance No. 23 of 2026) mandate **exclusive local storage within Bangladesh**, or does it permit **a synchronised real-time replica in Bangladesh** while maintaining a regional primary? If a copy suffices, does **Option B** (Regional Primary with a Bangladesh replica in Zone 4) meet 100% of the statutory requirement? |
| **Q3** | **Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) Status:** Is 1000FiX Services Limited, or any segment of its after-sales and logistics operations, capable of designation as a **Critical Information Infrastructure (CII)** under Section 15 of the Cyber Security Act / PDPA Section 28(3)? Furthermore, what statutory duties attach if an institutional **client** of the Company (e.g. licensed scheduled banks, mobile network operators, or defence contractors) is designated a CII facility? |
| **Q4** | **Large-Scale Transfer Thresholds & Notification:** What constitutes a **"large-scale transfer"** quantitatively under the Act and draft Transfer Rules for restricted personal data, and what mandatory notification, safeguard, and prior-approval duties attach to the Company? |
| **Q5** | **Statutory Deadline & Grace Period:** What compliance obligations are mandatory by **~13 May 2027** specifically (the expiry of the 12-month statutory transition period), as distinct from aspirational good governance practices? |
