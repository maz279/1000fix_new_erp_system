## 1. Background & Convening

The Chairman called the meeting to order, noting a quorum of Directors present. The Managing Director introduced the business case, Software Requirements Specification (SRS), and Technical Architecture for the complete digital transformation of 1000FiX Services Ltd into Bangladesh's first ISO 9001:2015 certified 8-Portal Integrated Smart Service & Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Platform (`IR-002`).

The Managing Director presented the Programme Charter (`D-001`), highlighting the necessity of formal governance committees, cross-functional architecture authority, and statutory appointments mandated by the newly enacted **Personal Data Protection Act 2026 (PDPA 2026)** and its Amendment Ordinance No. 23 of 2026.

---

## 2. Resolutions Adopted Unanimously

### Resolution 1: Formal Approval of ERP Transformation Charter (`D-001`)
> **IT WAS RESOLVED THAT** the Board of Directors hereby formally approves and adopts the **Integrated Smart ERP Platform Programme Charter (`D-001`)**, authorising the executive leadership to execute the 12-phase, 120-milestone transformation programme across all 67 operating modules, 8 public and partner portals, and nationwide service branch network.

### Resolution 2: Constitution of Programme Governance Bodies
> **IT WAS FURTHER RESOLVED THAT** the following programme governance bodies be immediately constituted with the delegated authority, compositions, and standing meeting cadences set forth below:

#### 2.1 Programme Steering Committee
- **Mandate:** Highest executive governance body for programme budget, schedule, vendor contracts, change requests exceeding 20 PW, and gate sign-offs (G0a through G9).
- **Composition:**
  1. Managing Director & CEO (Chairperson)
  2. Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
  3. Chief Operating Officer (COO)
  4. Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
  5. Head of Customer Operations & Service Delivery
  6. Programme Director / Lead Project Manager (Secretary)
- **Cadence:** Bi-weekly standing meeting.

#### 2.2 Architecture Review Board (ARB)
- **Mandate:** Technical governance authority responsible for ADR approvals (`AR-004`), technology baseline currency (`AR-013`), fitness function integrity (`AR-012`), interface contracts, and architectural change control under `GV-003`.
- **Composition:**
  1. Lead Enterprise Architect / Principal Systems Architect (Chairperson)
  2. Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
  3. Lead Security Architect / CISO
  4. Principal Data Architect
  5. Lead Frontend/UX Architect
  6. Lead Odoo Core Architect
- **Cadence:** Weekly standing session.

#### 2.3 Data Protection Forum (DP Forum)
- **Mandate:** Oversight of data privacy, compliance with statutory requirements under the Bangladesh Personal Data Protection Act 2026, data residency policies (`NFR-086`), privacy impact assessments (PIA), data subject rights (DSR) workflows, and audit logging.
- **Composition:**
  1. Chief Data Officer (Chairperson)
  2. Legal Counsel & Company Secretary
  3. Head of Information Security (CISO)
  4. Lead Enterprise Architect
  5. Customer Privacy & Compliance Officer
- **Cadence:** Monthly standing meeting (or within 24 hours of any regulatory notification/incident).

## 3. Statutory & Executive Appointments

### 3.1 Appointment of Statutory Chief Data Officer (CDO)
> **IT WAS RESOLVED THAT**, in accordance with Section 14 of the **Personal Data Protection Act 2026 (Act No. XII of 2026)**, the Board hereby appoints:
>
> **Mr. Tariqul Islam, CISA, CRISC, CDPSE**  
> *Vice President — Information Security, Governance, Risk & Compliance*  
> Employee ID: `FX-EMP-00142`  
>
> as the designated **Chief Data Officer (CDO)** of 1000FiX Services Limited, with immediate effect.
>
> **Statutory Powers & Remit:**
> - Independent supervisory authority over the processing of all Personal Data and Restricted Personal Data across all corporate databases, portals, and cloud environments.
> - Direct escalation path to the Board of Directors and Programme Steering Committee on any privacy or residency breach.
> - Official statutory liaison to the Data Protection Authority of Bangladesh.
> - Sole signatory authority for Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA) and cross-border transfer filings.
