## The finding

The SoW buys **2,930 requirements and 101 NFRs**; the measured estate baseline
is **3,389 and 122**. **0 of the 459-requirement difference are unbought by
accident** — the alignment audit (§4.1) established the 3,389 engineering
population is the contracted scope in substance. Unamended, a supplier
satisfying the contract in full leaves them unbuilt **and is entitled to
payment** (`B-11`).

## The resolution (two clauses, ready for the amendment)

1. **Scope-by-baseline clause (replaces fixed figures):**
   > "The Services cover the requirement population as maintained in the
   > requirements baseline (`RQ-001` §3.10.5, counting rule `CR-01`), as
   > re-stamped at each wave gate. Coverage, effort and completeness are
   > reported against that maintained baseline and no other denominator
   > (`CR-02`). At the date of this amendment the baseline stands at 3,389
   > requirements (3,347 adopted module/portal rows + 42 supplementary `SWR`)
   > and 122 non-functional requirements."

2. **NFR clause:** the NFR register is `RQ-006` as baselined (122,
   five-duplicate resolution in force), maintained under the same rule.

A fixed figure in a contract against a living baseline guarantees this dispute
recurs (P01-M08-T03's rationale, enacted in the clause itself).
