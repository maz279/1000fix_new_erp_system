## 1. Preamble & Recitals

**WHEREAS:**
1. The original Master Statement of Work `CO-003` cited fixed statutory scope counts of 2,930 functional requirements and 101 Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs).
2. The executable requirements scan and baseline alignment audit executed on 1 October 2026 (`CR-01` baseline counting rule) established that the true contracted scope consists of **3,389 requirements** (comprising 3,347 adopted domain and portal requirements plus 42 supplementary software requirements `SWR`) and **122 Non-Functional Requirements**.
3. Zero of the 459-requirement difference were excluded by intent; the engineering population represents the contracted functional scope in substance.
4. To permanently eliminate commercial exposure and avoid recurrent scope disputes at subsequent wave gates, Client and Implementation Partner agree to amend the Statement of Work with immediate effect.

---

## 2. Operative Amendments

### Clause 1: Replacement of Fixed Counts with Scope-by-Baseline Mechanism
Section 4.1 of `CO-003` is hereby deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following text:

> **"Section 4.1 — Requirements Baseline & Counting Principle:**
> The Services delivered under this Statement of Work cover the complete requirement population maintained within the official Requirements Baseline (`RQ-001` §3.10.5, counting rule `CR-01`), as re-stamped and verified by automated tools at each wave gate. Coverage, completeness, milestone acceptance, and billing shall be measured and reported against that maintained baseline and no other denominator (`CR-02`).
>
> At the date of this Amendment No. 1, the baseline stands at **3,389 requirements** (3,347 adopted module and portal rows plus 42 supplementary `SWR`) and **122 Non-Functional Requirements** (`RQ-006` as baselined). Neither party shall assert the superseded 2,930 or 101 figures for any delivery, acceptance, or commercial purpose."

### Clause 2: Effort Baseline Harmonisation
Section 6.3 of `CO-003` is hereby amended to incorporate the harmonised custom development effort:

> **"Section 6.3 — Development Effort Baseline:**
> The overall custom development effort baseline for custom units (`CD-Mnn-nn`) is formally restated at **1,842 Person-Weeks (PW)** (with the machine-readable register `cd-register.yaml` carrying 1,850 PW across 331 registered units, incorporating the 8 PW transcription reserve noted in the baseline audit). The Client acknowledges that portal development effort (298 PW across the 8 portals) is included within this harmonised figure."

### Clause 3: No Additional Fee Penalty
Implementation Partner confirms that the adoption of the 3,389 requirement baseline represents the true intended scope of the transformation programme, and no additional licensing fees or penalty surcharges attach to this baseline reconciliation.
