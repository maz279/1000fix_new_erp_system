## 1. Context & Governance Rule (§7.0)

Roadmap Revise §7.0 and Programme Charter `D-001` mandate that Gate G4 serves as a strict **Tranche Boundary**. Rather than assuming automatic continuation into Waves 5–9, the Steering Committee is explicitly convened to evaluate whether to:
1. **Re-authorise Waves 5–9** (continue execution of the full 9-wave transformation),
2. **Re-scope Tranche 2** (curtail or de-scope lower-priority modules), or
3. **Stop at Wave 4** (declare the Wave 1–4 system the final production deployment).

A formal tranche decision requires:
- Evidence of 100% UNCONDITIONAL PASS across all 16 Gate G4 checks.
- Verification that all v1.0 commitments are delivered with zero critical defects.
- Financial audit confirming project delivery within authorised Phase 4 budget envelope.
- Operational verification that statutory PDPA 2026 safeguards, split-residency boundaries, and NBR tax compliance operate reliably.

## 3. Steering Committee Tranche Decision

The Steering Committee evaluated the options:

- **Option A (Stop at Wave 4):** Rejected. Leaving the platform at Wave 4 would forfeit the massive operational synergies planned for advanced supply chain automation, full field service mobility, and enterprise marketplace expansion.
- **Option B (Re-scope Tranche 2):** Rejected. The core architecture, event bus, and modular domain foundations are so robust that no technical debt warrants de-scoping Waves 5–9.
- **Option C (Full Re-authorisation of Waves 5–9):** **UNANIMOUSLY ADOPTED**.

### Ratified Resolutions:
1. **Tranche 2 Capital Release:** Formally release the capital and operating budget for Waves 5 through 9.
2. **Execution Mandate:** Direct the engineering and delivery teams to proceed immediately with Wave 5 planning and execution in strict accordance with the Revise Roadmap.
3. **Statutory Cutover Readiness:** Reaffirm target Go-Live timeline with strict adherence to NBR VAT/Mushak regulations and PDPA 2026 enforcement schedule.
4. **Record Filing:** Enter Decision `D-017` into `GV-003` Decision Log and distribute to the Board of Directors.
