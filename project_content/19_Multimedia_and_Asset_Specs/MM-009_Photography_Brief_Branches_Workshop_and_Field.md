## 1. Purpose

The register's justification carries both halves in one line: *"Real photography of real branches
and technicians is a trust asset; it is also a consent and safety obligation."*

**The trust half.** A network of 51+ branches, 200–400 field technicians and eight divisions is
the company's most credible claim, and stock photography of a Western office says the opposite of
it. `MM-005` §4.2 makes the same argument for illustration; photography makes it more strongly and
costs more to get wrong.

**The obligation half, which is why this file has five reviewers from outside Brand.**

| Every frame containing… | Is also… | Governed by |
|---|---|---|
| An identifiable person | **Personal data being processed** | `LG-008` §4.2, `05-06` §6.3 |
| An employee | Personal data processed in an **employment relationship**, where consent is at its weakest | `LG-008` `P-10`, `O-02` here |
| A workshop bench | A record of whether the practice shown was safe and compliant | `SP-030`, `KB-013` |
| A customer's device or document | **`S2`/`S3` data**, and a disclosure if published | `05-02` §10, `MM-002` §4.4 |
| A branch interior | Premises security information | `LG-021` |
| A photographer's work | **Copyright owned by the photographer unless assigned in writing** | Copyright Act 2023 ‡ |

**Consequence of absence (`CP-03`).** A shoot commissioned without this brief produces a library
that cannot lawfully be published: no recorded consents, no releases in the metadata, unknown
copyright, an unsafe practice in frame, and a customer's device serial legible in the background —
with `05-06` §6.3 recording penalty exposure of **BDT 25 lakh, rising to BDT 50 lakh** and
enforcement mechanisms activating around **13 May 2027**, inside Wave 4, which is this file's own
wave.

**What this file is not.** It is not the privacy notice (`LG-008`), not the redaction standard
(`MM-002` §4.4 — cited, not restated), not illustration (`MM-005`), not video (`MM-006`, `MM-007`
— which cite §4.2 for their own consent), and not the partner co-branding guide (`EO-012`).

### 4.2 Consent — the hardest section

#### 4.2.1 What valid consent is here

`LG-008` §3 defines it: **a clear, explicit, specific and freely given positive indication of
agreement** (`NFR-091`). `CN-R2` adds that it is captured by an **affirmative act** — *"a
pre-ticked box, an inferred consent, or silence is not consent."* `CN-R3` makes withdrawal **as
easy as giving and immediately effective**.

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **PH-02** | **Every identifiable person in frame has a recorded, written consent obtained before the shutter, not after.** "Identifiable" includes a person recognisable from build, uniform, workplace or context even where the face is not visible |
| **PH-03** | **Consent is specific to stated uses and stated media.** A consent for internal communications is not a consent for a recruitment campaign. **The uses are listed on the form and nothing outside the list is permitted** (`LG-008` `PI-02`) |
| **PH-04** | **Consent is time-bounded.** A stated term — three years is the working default — after which the asset is retired or consent is re-obtained (`MM-012` §4.7) |
| **PH-05** | **Consent is withdrawable at any time, without reason and without consequence**, and withdrawal is honoured **immediately across every surface** (`CN-R3`). §4.7 states what happens then |
| **PH-06** | **The notice version presented is recorded with the consent** (`LG-008` `CN-R1`, `BRU-CPM-08`). **A consent recorded without it is invalid**, and this is the single most commonly missed field on a paper release form |
| **PH-07** | **An unrecorded consent is no consent.** An asset whose release status is empty is treated as **unreleased** and is not published (`MM-001` `MT-02`) |
| **PH-08** | **Consent records are held in `M24`** with the asset's `MM-012` record pointing to them — never in a photographer's filing cabinet (`CG-012` `EV-07`) |
| **PH-09** | **The consent form is bilingual and the Bengali is the operative version for most subjects.** `PS-03` makes Bengali the default for the field and branch populations; a consent a person did not read in a language they read fluently is not *"freely given"* in substance, whatever it is in form |
| **PH-10** | **The person is given a plain-language explanation before signing**: what is being photographed, where it may appear, for how long, how to withdraw, and who to contact. **In Bengali, verbally, by someone who is not the photographer** |

#### 4.2.2 The employee problem, stated rather than assumed

> **Consent given by an employee to their employer is the weakest form of consent there is**, and
> the reason is structural: the person asked is in a relationship where refusal has a perceived
> cost. `LG-008` `P-10` bases employment processing on **employment and social-security duties**,
> not on consent — and **photography for brand and communications purposes is not an employment
> duty.**

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **PH-11** | **Refusal is free and is visibly free.** The request is made by someone **outside the person's line management**, refusal is recorded as neutral, and **no list of who declined is circulated** |
| **PH-12** | **Participation is never scheduled as work.** A technician is not rostered to a shoot; they are asked |
| **PH-13** | **A branch or workshop manager cannot consent on behalf of their staff.** They consent to the **premises** (`PH-16`), not to the people |
| **PH-14** | **Withdrawal after employment ends is honoured on the same terms** (`PH-05`). A former employee's photograph on a careers page is the most common instance and the least defensible |
| **PH-15** | **Whether consent is the correct lawful basis at all for employee photography is a `LG-008` question and it is open** — see `O-02`. This file proceeds on consent because it is the most protective basis available, **not because it has been confirmed as the correct one** |

#### 4.2.3 Premises, customers and third parties

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **PH-16** | **Premises consent is obtained from the site owner** — the branch or workshop manager for a 1000FiX site, the property owner for a leased or third-party site. Recorded as an **IPTC property release** (`MM-001` §4.9) |
| **PH-17** | **A customer is not photographed.** Not in a branch, not at a doorstep, not in the background. **A branch shoot happens outside opening hours or in a closed area**, and this is a scheduling requirement, not a preference |
| **PH-18** | **A customer's device is not photographed** where it is identifiable — serial, asset tag, personalisation, damage pattern, contents on screen. A staged device from `MD-045`-equivalent company stock is used instead |
| **PH-19** | **No third party in frame**: a passer-by, a courier from another company, a neighbouring shop's signage, a competitor's vehicle |
| **PH-20** | **Signage identifying a customer's home or business is never in frame** (`MM-002` §4.4.3) |
| **PH-21** | **A shoot at a customer site is exceptional and requires the customer's own written consent obtained in advance**, with `PH-17`–`PH-20` still applying to everyone else present |

### 4.3 Safety on set

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **PH-22** | **Every frame shows compliant practice.** PPE correct and worn, isolation performed, tools correct, the SOP followed. **A published photograph is an instruction**: it tells 800+ employees what normal looks like, and it will be produced in an incident investigation |
| **PH-23** | **HSE approves the shot list before the shoot and reviews the selects before release, and may stop a shoot** — the same authority `MM-006` `VT-25` gives for video, stated identically so the two cannot diverge |
| **PH-24** | **No hazardous procedure is staged at a risk level the SOP would not accept.** Live electrical, refrigerant, battery and working-at-height subjects are photographed **de-energised, on a mock rig, or not at all** (`KB-013`, `SP-030`) |
| **PH-25** | **The shoot does not obstruct live work.** A photographer in a workshop aisle is a hazard; a photographer between a technician and an energised device is a serious one |
| **PH-26** | **A photograph showing a safety breach is deleted, not archived** — and the breach is reported under `SP-030` as an observation. Retaining it "for reference" makes the company's own library the evidence |

### 4.4 Confidentiality in frame

**`MM-002` §4.4 is the standard; this section is its photography-specific application.**

| Never in frame | Why |
|---|---|
| **A screen showing production data** | `MM-002` `SC-01`, `SC-29` — a monitor in the background is a screenshot nobody reviewed |
| **A document** — job card, invoice, delivery note, whiteboard, roster, price list | `MM-002` §4.4.1 |
| **A customer device serial, asset tag or IMEI** | `AP-012` §4.5.6, `MM-002` §4.4.3 |
| **A second device's identifiers in the background** | `AP-012` names this leak path explicitly |
| **A reflection** — in a screen, a window, a polished bench, a technician's glasses | The case that is always missed (`MM-002` §4.4.3) |
| **Access control** — keypads, key positions, safe locations, server-room layout, cabling | `LG-021`; premises security |
| **Brand-partner confidential material** — authorisation certificates, technical bulletins, parts pricing | 17+ agreements; `KB-010` |
| **A competitor's product or branding** | — |

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **PH-27** | **The set is cleared before the frame, not corrected after it.** `MM-002` `SC-23`'s principle applies: restricted content is **not captured**, not redacted. A redacted brand photograph is a photograph that should not have been taken |
| **PH-28** | **A photograph is never used as training data for any model**, extending `LG-008` §4.5.4 and `BRU-DTS-14` from recovered data to commissioned imagery of employees. **This is stated on the consent form**, because a subject who was not told is a subject who did not consent |

### 4.6 Rights, licensing and the Bangladesh position ‡

> ‡ **Every clause in this section rests on an authoritative secondary summary of the Copyright Act
> 2023 and is unverified against the enacted instrument.** `CG-012` `EV-02` prohibits relying on a
> secondary source for a legal position of consequence. **These clauses must be verified before the
> first photography purchase order is issued** — `O-03`.

| Position | Consequence for a purchase order |
|---|---|
| **Copyright in a photograph vests in the creator by default** ‡ | **The company owns nothing it has not taken by agreement.** A paid invoice is not an assignment |
| **A commissioning party may own the copyright where the agreement so provides** ‡ | **The assignment must be written, signed and specific**, and it must be in the purchase order or a schedule to it |
| **Moral rights remain with the author** ‡ — the right to claim authorship and to object to derogatory treatment | **Assignment does not buy the right to alter a photograph in any way the author could call derogatory.** This is why §4.7's retouching limits are also a rights position |
| **Term runs 60 years from the death of the author** ‡ | The asset outlives every current arrangement, which is why `MM-012` §4.5 records rights permanently |

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **PH-32** | **Rights are assigned in writing before the shoot**, not negotiated at delivery (`MM-001` `DL-05`). A supplier who will not assign is not engaged |
| **PH-33** | **The licence is stated as territory, term, media and purpose**, and **worldwide, perpetual, all-media** is what is bought — because a photograph found on a partner's site in five years cannot be re-licensed retrospectively |
| **PH-34** | **Attribution and credit are agreed in writing**, given the moral-rights position. Where the supplier requires a credit line it is recorded in the IPTC Credit Line field and honoured (`MM-001` §4.9) |
| **PH-35** | **Supplier portfolio use is refused by default** and granted only per image, in writing, and never for a frame containing an employee or a premises interior |
| **PH-36** | **Model and property release status is recorded in the IPTC Extension fields inside the file** (`MM-001` `MT-02`), so the release travels with the asset and an empty field reads as unreleased |
| **PH-37** | **Third-party material in frame carries its own clearance** — music in a behind-the-scenes cut, artwork on a wall, a brand-partner mark |

### 4.7 Technical specification, retouching and withdrawal

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **PH-38** | Capture in **camera raw**, deliver a **16-bit TIFF master** and sRGB derivatives (`MM-001` §4.2 class A6); long edge **≥ 4000 px** |
| **PH-39** | **Retouching is limited to exposure, colour, straightening and cropping.** **Nothing that changes a fact may be altered** — PPE cannot be added, a hazard cannot be removed, a person cannot be composited in, a serial cannot be painted out **after** the frame is taken (`PH-27`) |
| **PH-40** | **Generative fill, object removal and any synthetic alteration are prohibited** on any photograph the company publishes as documentary. Where an image is composited or synthetic, **it is labelled as an illustration and moves to `MM-005`** |
| **PH-41** | **Location metadata is stripped from every published file** (`MM-001` `MT-03`). EXIF GPS on a branch or a technician's location is a security and privacy disclosure that survives every copy |
| **PH-42** | Camera serial, device identifiers and author software strings are stripped; **rights and release metadata is re-stamped**, never left to survive stripping by accident (`MM-001` `MT-05`) |
| **PH-43** | **Consent withdrawal triggers withdrawal of every asset containing that person**, across every surface, cache and printed run in circulation — and **the reprint of any material in production**. `MM-012` §4.7 holds the mechanism; **it must be able to answer "which assets contain this person" in one query**, which is what `PH-36`'s metadata is for |
| **PH-44** | **Consent expiry (`PH-04`) triggers the same sweep**, automatically, without waiting for a person to ask |
| **PH-45** | **Retention follows [`LG-009`](../10_Legal_Policy_and_Compliance/LG-009_Data_Retention_and_Deletion_Schedule.md)**; unused frames of identifiable people are deleted at the end of the commission (`PH-29`) |

### 4.8 Deliverable specification

**The `CG-009` §3 required addition for Category 19.**

| Attribute | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Master format** | **Camera raw + 16-bit TIFF**, unretouched raw retained |
| **Derived formats** | AVIF + WebP + JPEG at three widths (`MM-001` §4.4); print-profile TIFF on request |
| **Dimensions** | Long edge **≥ 4000 px** |
| **Colour** | Capture **Adobe RGB or wider**, deliver **sRGB with the profile embedded** (`MM-001` `CS-02`) |
| **Compression** | Derived JPEG q80, **≤ 300 KB at 1600 px** |
| **Retouching** | Exposure, colour, straighten, crop **only** (`PH-39`); **no generative or synthetic alteration** (`PH-40`) |
| **Metadata — mandatory** | IPTC Core: creator, credit line, copyright notice, **rights usage terms**, web statement of rights · IPTC Extension: **model release status, property release status, licensor** · XMP: title, description, `dc:relation` = commission reference |
