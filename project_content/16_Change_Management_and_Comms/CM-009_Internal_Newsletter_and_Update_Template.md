# CM-009 — Internal Newsletter and Update Template

> **Scope note.** This file owns **the format and the cadence rules**. It does not own the story
> (`CM-001`), the calendar (`CM-003`), the wave assets it summarises (`CM-005`) or the recognition
> copy it carries (`CM-017`). The newsletter **quotes and links**; it does not author.
>
> **Language note.** This file is `bilingual: no` because it is a **template** — the same
> treatment `CG-009` §3 gives `NT-001`, `DT-001` and `HC-002`. **Every instance produced from it
> is bilingual.** `NL-11` makes that binding, and it is the one rule in this file that must not be
> read as advisory.

---

## 1. Purpose

The register's justification is exact: *"Silence between waves is filled with rumour; a
predictable cadence prevents it."*

Thirty-six months is long enough for a workforce to stop believing that a programme is happening.
`RSK-003` — programme fatigue — is rated High and its mitigation is *"visible wave-by-wave value
delivery"*. The monthly update is the only mechanism in the programme that reaches all 500–900
internal staff regardless of which wave they are in.

**Consequence of absence (`CP-03`).**

| Failure | Consequence |
|---|---|
| No cadence | Staff in waves 5–9 hear nothing for two years and conclude the programme was abandoned |
| Cadence that skips quiet months | A missing issue becomes a signal, and the signal is always read as bad |
| No fixed format | Each issue is read from the beginning to find out what it contains |
| No feedback loop | Questions raised at the forum disappear, and the forum stops receiving them |
| A newsletter of achievements only | It becomes marketing, and marketing to your own staff is discounted within three issues |
| English-only instances | It reaches head office and not the 51+ branches it exists for |
| Authored per issue from scratch | It is the first thing dropped when the Change Lead is busy — which is every month |

## 4. Content

### 4.1 The six sections

**`NL-01`: six sections, in order, every issue, one page.** A seventh section is not added; a
section with nothing in it says so.

| # | Section | Length | Rule |
|:--:|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Where we are** | 40 words | Wave, month, what is live, at how many sites. **Counted, never characterised** |
| **2** | **What is coming next, and when it reaches you** | 60 words | Named audiences and sites. If it reaches nobody next month, say so |
| **3** | **What we heard, and what happened about it** | **80 words — the largest section** | Questions from the champion forum and the floor, with the outcome. **Mandatory** |
| **4** | **One thing that is working** | 40 words | A specific, verifiable thing at a named site. Not a benefit claim (`NR-08`) |
| **5** | **Recognition** | 40 words | Named people, specifically what they did (`CM-017`) |
| **6** | **Where to ask** | 20 words | The champion, the change inbox, and one named person |

> **`NL-02` — section 3 is the reason the newsletter is read.** An update that reports only what
> the programme has done is a press release. An update that reports what the workforce said, and
> what happened as a result, is evidence that speaking has an effect. It is also the section that
> makes `CM-002` §4.7 resistance tracking visible to the people who generated it.

### 4.2 The quiet-month rule

**`NL-03`: an issue is published every month, including months with no news.** The quiet-issue
form:

| Section | Quiet-month content |
|---|---|
| 1 | *"Wave [n] is in build. [x] sites are live. Nothing changed for you this month."* |
| 2 | *"Nothing reaches [audience] before [month]."* — with the date |
| 3 | **Unchanged — always populated.** There are always questions |
| 4 | A working thing from a site already live |
| 5 | Unchanged |
| 6 | Unchanged |

> **`NL-04` — "nothing changed for you this month" is a valid and valuable message.** It is
> precisely the information a technician in a wave-6 branch wants and cannot otherwise get.

### 4.3 Content rules

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **`NL-05`** | **One page.** A4 printed, or one screen without scrolling on a mid-range phone |
| **`NL-06`** | **Reading level per `CG-011`**; sentences under 20 words; no programme jargon (`NR-11`) |
| **`NL-07`** | Every number carries its source and date (`CM-R8`); no percentage without its denominator |
| **`NL-08`** | **No benefit claim outside the permitted set for the current gate** (`NR-08`, `CM-001` §4.5) |
| **`NL-09`** | No individual is named in a negative context, ever. Section 5 names people only positively |
| **`NL-10`** | No content about pay, grading, headcount or appraisal. Those are HR communications and are routed there (`NR-10`) |
| **`NL-11`** | **Every issue is published in Bengali and English on the same day.** An English-only issue is not published |
| **`NL-12`** | Published on a **fixed working day of the month**, decided once and not moved |
| **`NL-13`** | Distributed to the branch noticeboard as well as digitally — `SK-09` means email does not reach `S07`–`S10` |
| **`NL-14`** | Archived. Every issue is retrievable, because section 3 is a record of commitments made |

### 4.4 The issue frame

```
1000FiX ERP Programme — Update [n]                        [Month Year]
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHERE WE ARE
[Wave n, month n of 36]. Live at [n] sites. [What is live.]

WHAT IS COMING NEXT
[Capability] reaches [audience] at [sites] in [month].
Nothing reaches [other audiences] before [month].

WHAT WE HEARD — AND WHAT HAPPENED
"[Question, as asked.]"  →  [What was done. Who did it. When.]
"[Question, as asked.]"  →  [Still open. Owner. Expected by.]

ONE THING THAT IS WORKING
[Specific, at a named site, verifiable.]

RECOGNITION
[Name, site] — [specifically what they did.]

WHERE TO ASK
Your champion: [name]. Change inbox: [address]. Or ask [named person].
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Also on your branch noticeboard · এই সংখ্যাটি বাংলাতেও পাওয়া যাচ্ছে
```

> **`NL-15` (recorded in §4.7): an open question is reported as open, with an owner and a date.**
> Reporting only resolved questions turns section 3 into the achievements section, which is what
> section 4 is for.

## 6. Maintenance

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Review cadence | **Every wave boundary** — format changes at most once per wave, never mid-wave |
| Trigger for out-of-cycle review | Readership collapse · section 3 running empty for two issues (a forum failure, not a newsletter failure) · a `CG-011` finding |
| Owner | Change Lead |
| Change log | `RF-006`; a section added or removed is MAJOR |
| Enforcement | `NL-01`…`NL-16`; `NL-11` blocks publication of an English-only issue |
| Improvement loop | **The measure is whether section 3 questions decline in repetition, not whether readership rises.** The same question appearing for a third month means the answer is not reaching people, whatever the open rate says |
