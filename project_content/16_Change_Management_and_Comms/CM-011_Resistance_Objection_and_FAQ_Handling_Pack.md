# CM-011 — Resistance, Objection and FAQ Handling Pack

> **Scope note.** This file owns **the objections and the approved responses**. It does not own the
> narrative (`CM-001`), the tracking mechanism (`CM-002` §4.7), the delivery of responses in a room
> (`CM-010`), or any HR policy. **Where a response touches pay, grading, monitoring or headcount it
> is approved by the HR Director, not by Content Design**, and this file records that constraint
> rather than working around it.
>
> **Honesty note.** Several responses in §4.3 and §4.4 are not reassuring. They are approved
> because they are true. `OJ-02` states the principle: **an objection with no honest good answer is
> escalated, not answered.**

---

## 1. Purpose

The register's justification is exact: *"Unaddressed objections become quiet non-adoption, which is
far harder to detect and reverse."*

Open resistance is a gift — it is visible, it names its reason, and it can be answered. Quiet
non-adoption looks like compliance: the app is installed, the login happens, and the real work
continues on paper in the van. It is discovered at the gate, when `G2-01` requires the app *"in
daily use by 100% of Dhaka technicians"* and the usage data does not support it.

**Consequence of absence (`CP-03`).**

| Failure | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Objections not anticipated | The first honest question is answered by improvisation, in front of an audience |
| Dishonest reassurance | The reassurance is disproved by events, and every subsequent statement is discounted |
| No HR boundary | A change speaker makes a commitment about pay or headcount that the Company has not made |
| Monitoring objection unaddressed | `RSK-008` materialises as quiet non-use, not as refusal |
| No escalation for genuine grievance | A person who is materially worse off is repeatedly "communicated at" |
| Responses not translated | The objection is raised in Bengali and answered in English, which is itself an answer |
| No logging | The same objection is re-answered at 51 sites and nobody learns |
| Written for the programme rather than the person | Every response begins "the programme aims to…" and none of them lands |

## 4. Content

### 4.1 The taxonomy

`CM-002` §4.7 classifies objections into four types. **`OJ-01`: the type determines the response,
and three of the four types are not communication problems.**

| Type | Meaning | Correct response | Wrong response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Capability** | *"I can't."* | **Training, job aid, practice, device** | Explaining why the change is good |
| **Clarity** | *"I don't understand."* | **Information** — this is the only type communication fixes | Repeating the same message louder |
| **Belief** | *"I don't accept the reason."* | **Evidence, and involvement** — put the objector into the design or the UAT (`RSK-009` mitigation) | Assertion |
| **Interest** | *"I understand, and I am worse off."* | **Escalation** (`SK-15`) — and honesty about what will and will not change | Persuasion |

> **`OJ-02` — an `Interest` objection is never answered with a better message.** A person who is
> genuinely worse off and is repeatedly persuaded rather than heard stops raising it and stops
> using the system. The response is: acknowledge it precisely, say what can and cannot be changed,
> escalate it to the Steering Committee, and **come back with the outcome whatever it is.**

### 4.2 Response rules

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **`OJ-03`** | **Every response is true.** A response that is currently true but will stop being true is not used |
| **`OJ-04`** | **Answer the question that was asked**, not the adjacent question with a better answer |
| **`OJ-05`** | Responses touching pay, grading, monitoring, headcount, discipline or appraisal are **approved by the HR Director** and are delivered by, or explicitly attributed to, HR |
| **`OJ-06`** | Where the answer is *"we have not decided"*, that is the answer, with **who decides and by when** |
| **`OJ-07`** | Where the answer is *"yes, and it is not going to change"*, that is the answer. Softening it produces a second conversation later, in worse conditions |
| **`OJ-08`** | No response begins with the programme. It begins with the person's situation |
| **`OJ-09`** | **Two sentences, then the route.** Champions are not expected to hold an argument (`NW-04`, `CM-005` K6) |
| **`OJ-10`** | A response is recorded when used, with the site and the audience group, and fed to `CM-002` §4.7 |

### 4.3 The twelve core objections

Ordered by how much damage the wrong answer does.

---

**`OB-01` — "Is this going to cost jobs?"**
*Type: Interest / Belief · Raised by: everyone · Approved by: HR Director*

> **Response.** *"The programme is required to augment roles, not to displace staff. That is a
> written constraint on it — `CON-10` — and it is not something the programme can change on its
> own. If the Company ever decided otherwise, that decision would be taken and communicated by the
> Company, not by this programme, and you would hear it from HR and not from a system launch. What
> the programme does change is the paper around your job."*

**What must not be said:** *"No one will lose their job."* The programme cannot bind future
Company decisions, and `NR-10` prohibits it. **Escalate** if the objector says they have already
been told otherwise by a manager — that is a contradiction under `NR-03`.

---

**`OB-02` — "Is this so you can watch us?"**
*Type: Belief / Interest · Raised by: `R10`–`R12` technicians, `R16` riders · Approved by: HR Director + Legal*

> **Response.** *"The app records where a job was done, when it started and stopped, and what
> parts were used — because the customer, the brand and the invoice all need that. It is the same
> information you write on the paper form today, with a location and a time attached. Your manager
> sees your jobs and your performance, as they do now. Here is exactly what is recorded and who
> can see it — [the disclosure]. If something on that list is not in it, we have got it wrong and
> I want to know."*

**Rules.**
- **`OJ-11`: this objection is answered with a disclosure, not with a reassurance.** A written,
  role-specific list of what is captured, why, how long it is kept, and who can see it — from
  `LG-020` and `05-04`. Anything less is heard as evasion, correctly.
- The disclosure is a **legal obligation as well as a change one.** `RSK-032` records the Personal
  Data Protection Act 2026, with enforcement from around 13 May 2027, and `DEP-34` records that
  PDPA-compliant transparency notice text does not yet exist (`O-02`).
- **What must not be said:** *"Nobody is watching you."* GPS tracking, geofenced clock-in and time
  tracking are in `P02`'s specified capability set. Denying it is disproved by the first shift.

---

**`OB-03` — "This is more work, not less."**
*Type: Interest, and often correct · Raised by: `R08` counter, `R10`–`R12` technicians*

> **Response.** *"For the first two weeks, yes — you are learning it and you are recording in two
> places during the parallel run. After that, some things are genuinely faster and some are not:
> intake is designed to be faster than today and we measure it; recording evidence on a job is
> more than you do now, and it replaces the form you fill in at the end of the day. If it is still
> slower for you after a month, tell your champion — that is a defect and we will look at it."*

**`OJ-12`: this objection is answered with a timeframe and a threshold, never with "you'll get
used to it."** The parallel-run double-entry cost is real, unresourced (`DM-014` `O-03`,
`CM-007` `O-06`), and staff will experience it before anyone tells them about it unless `HY-07`
is honoured.

---

**`OB-04` — "What happens to my incentive?"**
*Type: Interest · Approved by: HR Director — no other function may answer this*

> **Response.** *"The incentive scheme itself is not changed by this programme. What changes is
> that the work it is calculated from is recorded accurately, and you can see your own figure as
> it accrues instead of finding out at month end. If the scheme itself changes, that is an HR
> decision, HR will announce it, and it will not arrive inside a system release."*

**`OJ-13`: no programme speaker states or implies a change to incentive value.** `M57` calculates;
HR decides. Where a technician's recorded output falls because the recording is now accurate, that
is a real and foreseeable consequence and is escalated to HR **before** the wave, not after.

---

**`OB-05` — "The system will show I've been doing it wrong."**
*Type: Interest · Raised by: storekeepers, cashiers, workshop supervisors, branch managers*

> **Response.** *"The first count and the first close will show variances. Those variances existed
> before; they were just not visible. The opening position is a baseline, not an accusation — and
> the Company knows that stock has been tracked in spreadsheets and cash reconciled by hand.
> What matters is the position from the baseline forward."*

**`OJ-14`: the amnesty position must be confirmed by the CFO and the Operations Director before
this response is used.** An assurance about how opening variances will be treated is a management
commitment, not a communications line. Recorded as `O-01`.

---

**`OB-06` — "My branch is different."**
*Type: Belief · Raised by: `R06` branch managers*

> **Response.** *"Some of it is — geography, mix, staffing. Most of what feels different is a
> local workaround for a problem the system removes. Tell us the specific thing that does not fit,
> and if it is real it becomes a configuration or a change request. If it is a workaround, we will
> show you what replaces it."*

---

**`OB-07` — "The old system worked."**
*Type: Belief · Raised by: long-tenured staff across every function*

> **Response.** *"It works for the branch you can see. It does not work across 51 of them —
> 15–20% of warranty money is never recovered, month-end takes 15–20 days, and van stock is not
> tracked anywhere. Those are measured, not opinions. What worked locally is exactly what we want
> to keep, which is why we ask you what your workarounds are for."*

**`OJ-15`: figures cited in a response carry the same sourcing obligation as any other claim**
(`CM-R8`, BRD §6.2 and §6.3).

---

**`OB-08` — "I'm not good with technology."**
*Type: Capability — **not** an objection to answer with information*

> **Response.** *"You do not have to be. Training is in Bengali, hands-on, before you get access,
> and your champion is on site. If it is still hard after training, that is our design problem —
> tell your champion and it goes on the list."*

**`OJ-16`: `Capability` objections are routed to L&D and to the design team, never answered with
persuasion.** A `Capability` objection answered with a benefit statement is how `RSK-008` becomes
real.

---

**`OB-09` — "What if it goes down?"**
*Type: Clarity · Raised by: everyone, correctly*

> **Response.** *"The technician app works with no signal for a full day and syncs when it
> reconnects. At the counter, there is a defined manual fallback for every critical process and
> you will be trained on it. During cutover there is a paper contingency with numbered forms, and
> what you write on paper is keyed in afterwards — nothing you do is lost."*

**Depends on [`OP-014`](../17_Operations_Support_and_Runbooks/OP-014_Manual_Fallback_Procedure_Set.md), which is written, bilingual, and
carries the four sentences a counter agent says during an outage — in both languages.** What it does
**not** yet have is the physical pack: `OP-014-OI-02` records the fallback pack as **unfunded and
undistributed** across 51+ sites. The response is therefore true about the procedure and not yet true
about the forms (`O-03`).

---

**`OB-10` — "Why now, in the middle of everything else?"**
*Type: Belief · Raised by: managers and head office*

> **Response.** *"Because nine of the fourteen things this company has committed to selling have
> no system at all today, and building them later means rebuilding finance and service twice. It
> is nine waves over three years precisely so that no quarter absorbs all of it."*

---

**`OB-11` — "Nobody asked us."**
*Type: Belief · Raised by: branch and field staff*

> **Response.** *"Some of you have not been asked yet, and that is fair. Super-users and champions
> are named per site and per module, the field pilot takes working technicians before rollout, and
> UAT acceptance is signed by a business owner, not by the programme. If you want to be in it, tell
> your manager — we need more people than we have."*

**`OJ-17`: this response creates an obligation.** A person who volunteers after hearing it and is
never contacted has been given a worse answer than silence. The volunteer list is held by the
Change Lead and every name receives a reply.

---

**`OB-12` — "Will the reports be used against my branch?"**
*Type: Interest · Raised by: `R06` branch managers · Approved by: Operations Director*

> **Response.** *"The branch league table is real and it ranks all 51+ branches. It is also the
> first time your branch's numbers will be comparable rather than assembled by hand. The dashboard
> is designed as your tool — you see your position before anyone else does, and you are involved
> in choosing the measures."*

`06-01` §7 states the mitigation directly: *"position dashboards as their tool, not surveillance;
involve in KPI design."* **`OJ-18` (recorded in §4.8): branch managers are involved in KPI design
before the league table is published**, or the response is not usable.

### 4.5 The FAQ bank

**`OJ-19` (recorded in §4.8): the internal FAQ is a living bank, not a document.** Structure:

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Question | **As asked**, in the asker's words, in the language asked |
| Short answer | Two sentences (`OJ-09`) |
| Full answer | Where the champion needs more |
| Type | `Capability` / `Clarity` / `Belief` / `Interest` |
| Approver | Named function; HR for anything in `OJ-05`'s list |
| Valid until | A date or a wave — **an answer with no expiry becomes wrong silently** |
| Times asked, sites | Feeds `CM-005` `V6` and `CM-009` section 3 |
| Status | Approved · Escalated · **Unanswerable — and why** |

> **The bank includes an `Unanswerable` state deliberately.** `OB-01` under a future
> restructuring, `OB-04` under an unannounced scheme change, and `OB-05` without the `O-01`
> amnesty position are all currently unanswerable, and recording them as such is more useful than
> an approved answer that is not true.

## 7. Open Items

| # | Item | Owner | Needed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`O-01`** | **The opening-variance position is undecided.** `OB-05` is raised by storekeepers, cashiers, workshop supervisors and branch managers — the four groups who must produce the opening balances the whole migration depends on.
