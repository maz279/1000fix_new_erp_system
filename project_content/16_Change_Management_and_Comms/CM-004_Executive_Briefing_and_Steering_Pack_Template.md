# CM-004 — Executive Briefing and Steering Pack Template

> **Scope note.** This file owns **the shape of the pack and the rules for what may appear in it**.
> It does not own the programme data (`06-01`, `06-02`, the PMO's plan), the risk register
> (`00-04` §1, `RF-011` for content risk), or the decision log (`RF-006`, and `00-05` **which does
> not exist** — finding `AF-04`).
>
> **Template note.** This is a *content* template governed by `TPL_Content_File_Front_Matter.md`
> (`CG-009` §3 assigns all 18 `CM` files to template 1). The pack it specifies is a produced
> artefact, not a `_templates/` entry — `TR-09` reserves the `TPL_` prefix for authoring templates.

---

## 1. Purpose

The register's justification is exact: *"The charter awaits Steering Committee approval; the
committee needs a consistent decision surface."*

`00-04` §5.3 puts the Steering Committee at **monthly** for thirty-six months. That is thirty-six
meetings, each an hour or two, in which seven executives decide gate passage, scope change and
risk acceptance for a programme of 67 modules and 12 portals. The pack is the entire interface
between the programme and that authority.

**Consequence of absence (`CP-03`).**

| Failure | Consequence |
|---|---|
| A different pack shape each month | The first twenty minutes go on orientation; decisions are taken in the last five |
| Decisions buried in narrative | The committee discusses and does not decide; the decision reappears next month |
| No decision-by date | Every decision is equally urgent, so none is |
| Status without a baseline | "Green" means whatever the author felt |
| Risk section restated from the register | The register and the pack diverge, and the committee acts on the copy |
| Benefits reported before they are measured | The committee approves a gate on a claim `NR-08` prohibits |
| No record of what was decided | Decision `D-14` is cited by two audits and there is no decision log — `AF-04`, open |
| Bad news arriving as a surprise | The committee's response is to demand more reporting, which consumes the delivery capacity that caused the problem |

## 4. Content

### 4.1 The nine sections, in order

**`EP-01`: the order is fixed and decisions come second.** Not last, where the meeting has run
out of time, and not first, where they arrive without context.

| # | Section | Length | Purpose | Owner |
|:--:|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Position in one page** | 1 page, hard limit | Where the programme is, in numbers a director already knows | Programme Manager |
| **2** | **Decisions required this meeting** | 1 page + one page per decision | The reason the meeting exists | Programme Manager |
| **3** | **Wave progress and the next gate** | 1–2 pages | Burn-up against the wave, gate criteria status | Programme Manager |
| **4** | **Risk — Critical and High only** | 1 page | `00-04` §1.5 requires Critical risks reviewed **every meeting** | Risk owners, named |
| **5** | **Adoption and change** | 1 page | The programme's second-largest failure mode (`00-04` §8) | Change Lead |
| **6** | **Benefits tracking** | 1 page | Against baseline, with the measurement date | Benefit owners |
| **7** | **Budget and effort** | 1 page | Spend, forecast, and the standing TCO restatement | CFO / PMO |
| **8** | **Escalations and dependencies** | ≤ 1 page | Items the committee must unblock, with the person who owns each | PMO |
| **9** | **Decisions taken — record** | Appended after the meeting | Closes the loop; feeds the decision log | Programme Office |

> **`EP-02` — the pack is capped at ten pages plus decision annexes.** A pack that grows to
> thirty pages is read by nobody and is defended by the fact that everything was disclosed.

### 4.2 Section 2 — the decision item format

**`EP-03`: every decision item carries all seven fields. An item missing any of them is not
tabled.**

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Decision required** | One sentence, in the imperative. *"Approve X"*, *"Direct a descope of Y"* — never *"Discuss Z"* |
| **Authority** | The authority from `00-04` §5.2. If it is not the Steering Committee's, it does not belong in this section |
| **Decide by** | A date, with the consequence of missing it |
| **Options** | Two or three, each with cost, schedule, risk and benefit impact |
| **Recommendation** | The programme's recommendation, stated. A pack that presents options without a recommendation is asking the committee to do the analysis |
| **Consequence of no decision** | What happens if the item is deferred a month |
| **Reversibility** | Whether the decision can be revisited, and at what cost |

**`EP-04`: an item deferred twice is escalated with the deferral history attached.** Three
deferrals is a decision — to do nothing — and is recorded as one.

**The standing decision backlog at issue.** `00-04` §6 carries fifteen decisions; these are
already overdue or imminent and appear in Section 2 until closed:

| # | Decision | Authority | Status |
|---|---|---|:--:|
| **`D-01`** | Approve the expanded scope, budget and 36-month horizon | Board / CEO | **Open — blocks kickoff and all communication (`CA-17`)** |
| **`D-02`** | Confirm and protect super-user availability | CEO + department heads | **Open — `RSK-001`, Critical** |
| **`D-13`** | Approve the split-residency data architecture | CTO + CFO + Legal | **Open — "the most urgent open decision in the programme"** |
| **`D-14`** | Accept the restated provisional TCO, or direct a descope | Board / CEO / CFO | **Open — the Board holds a figure the audit found 60–80% low** |
| **`D-15`** | Significant data fiduciary classification; appoint a CDO | CEO + Legal | Open — W4 minus 3 months |
| `D-03` | Marketplace principal-vs-agent and commission model | CEO + CFO + Legal | Open — W7 minus 3 months |
| `D-04` | Legal entity for rental and refurbished resale | CFO + Legal | Open — W6 minus 3 months |
| `D-11` | Technician device procurement model | Ops Director + CFO | Open — **W2 minus 2 months; `RSK-008` depends on it** |

### 4.3 Section 1 — position in one page

`EP-05`: the same eleven figures, in the same order, every month. Movement is the message.

| Figure | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Wave and month | `06-01` §3 | Against plan, with variance in weeks |
| Requirements delivered / in wave | PMO burn-up | **Denominator stated** — see `O-01` |
| Gate criteria met / total for the next gate | `06-02` §4 | Counted, never characterised |
| Sites live / 51+ | Rollout record | Counted |
| Spend to date / forecast / envelope | Finance | Against `CON-01` |
| Critical risks open | `00-04` §1.5 | Count and movement |
| Decisions open / overdue | §4.2 | Count and oldest age |
| Adoption — worst-performing role or branch | `CM-016` | **Worst, not average** |
| Support volume trend | `OP-003` themes | Direction only |
| Content readiness for the next wave | Master Index §12 | **`P0` and `P1` content complete for the wave about to start** — audit check `G3` |
| Translation backlog | Localisation | Days of `G4` queue |

> **`EP-06` — the adoption figure is the worst performer, never the average.** An average
> conceals the branch that has stopped using the system, which is the only branch the committee
> can act on.

### 4.4 Section 4 — risk

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **`EP-07`** | **Only Critical and High risks appear.** `00-04` §1.5 requires Critical reviewed every meeting and High at each gate; Medium is a quarterly register review and does not consume Steering time |
| **`EP-08`** | Each risk is presented **by its named owner**, not by the PMO on their behalf (`00-04` §1.5) |
| **`EP-09`** | The pack **references** the register; it does not restate it. Where the pack and `00-04` §1 differ, the register governs |
| **`EP-10`** | A risk whose mitigation has not moved in two meetings is presented as an **escalation**, not as a risk |

The five Critical risks are standing items until downgraded: **`RSK-001`** super-user
availability, **`RSK-002`** scope creep, **`RSK-008`** technician adoption, **`RSK-022`**
marketplace tax and liability, **`RSK-023`** marketplace quality — plus **`RSK-032`**, **`RSK-033`**
and **`RSK-036`** added by the data-protection and cost-restatement findings.

### 4.5 Section 6 — benefits

**`EP-11`: a benefit is reported only against a captured baseline, with the measurement date.**
`00-04` §8 lists *"benefits are never measured"* as one of five ways the programme fails, and
names the mechanism: *"if baselines are not captured before each wave, no benefit can be claimed
honestly."*

| Column | Rule |
|---|---|
| Measure | Named KPI from `06-02`, cited by identifier |
| Baseline | The pre-wave value, **with the date it was captured** |
| Current | Measured, with the date |
| Target | From `06-02` |
| Status | Movement, not a colour |
| Claim permitted | **Yes / No** per `CM-001` §4.5 — a benefit may be *reported internally* before it may be *claimed externally* |

> **`EP-12` — internal reporting and external claiming are different permissions.** The pack may
> report a measure trending toward target; `NR-08` still prohibits announcing it until the gate
> criterion is met. Conflating the two is how a programme's own reporting becomes its
> communications risk.

### 4.6 Section 7 — budget, and the standing restatement

**`EP-13`: the TCO restatement appears in every pack until the Board has taken `D-14`.**

`00-02` §2 is explicit that the figure *"is provisional and must not be presented to the Board as
final until all 67 module specifications are written"*, and that it *"will move again"*. The
budget section therefore carries three lines permanently:

| Line | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Approved business case | $455K–$780K (v2.0 basis, 834 PW over 30 modules) | The figure the Board holds |
| Restated provisional TCO | **$620K–$1,050K** | `00-02` §2 — provisional, re-baselined each category |
| **Not in either figure** | **Content effort 520–775 PW** | Master Index §7; audit finding **AF-09**, escalated and unresolved |

> **`EP-14` — AF-09 is raised at the same agenda item as `D-14`, never separately.** The Master
> Index §7 says so directly. Two separate variance items invite two separate partial decisions.

### 4.7 The gate report

Produced per wave, not monthly (`00-04` §5.5). Structure:

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| **Criterion-by-criterion evidence** | Every criterion for that gate from `06-02` §4, with its evidence artefact named. `G1` has 12; `G4` has 10 |
| Conditional passes | Any partial pass **recorded as conditional with a dated remediation plan** (`06-02` §4) |
| Content readiness | `P0` and `P1` content complete for the wave (audit check `G3`) |
| Migration reconciliation | `DM-013` sign-offs; `DM-014` `C6` |
| Adoption position | `CM-016` measures; the worst performer |
| Hypercare readiness | [`OP-002`](../17_Operations_Support_and_Runbooks/OP-002_Hypercare_Plan_and_Exit_Criteria.md) §4.2 entry conditions, §4.8 exit criteria |
| Recommendation | Go / No-Go / Conditional, from the Programme Director |

**`EP-15`: a gate report states the criteria that were not met before the ones that were.**

### 4.8 The Board quarterly extract

`EP-16`: **the Board extract is derived from the last three Steering packs, never authored
separately.** Two independently written accounts of the same programme will diverge, and the
divergence surfaces at the worst possible moment.

| Extract section | Derived from |
|---|---|
| Investment position | Section 7, three months |
| Benefits realisation | Section 6, three months |
| Risk — Critical only | Section 4 |
| Strategic alignment | `00-02` §1 and §9 |
| Decisions for the Board | Section 2, filtered to Board authority (`00-04` §5.2) |

### 4.9 What may not appear in the pack

| Prohibited | Why |
|---|---|
| A completion percentage without its denominator | `AUDIT-014` TS-01; three current requirement totals |
| A traffic-light status with no defined threshold | "Amber" absorbs bad news |
| A benefit claim ahead of its gate | `NR-08` |
| A risk restated from the register with different wording | `EP-09` |
| An action item with no owner or date | Becomes a standing item |
| A slide of screenshots in place of a demonstration | `06-01` §5: *"every sprint review demonstrates working software, never slides"* |
| Any figure without a source and a verification date | `CM-R8`, `CG-012` |
| Personal performance data about a named individual | `CM-017` `RN-09`; monitoring boundary |

### 4.10 Distribution and pre-read

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Issue | **5 working days before the meeting** (`CM-003` §4.2) |
| Format | One PDF, one page-one summary; no attachments requiring a login to read |
| Pre-read expectation | Sections 1 and 2 read before the meeting; the meeting starts at Section 2 |
| Classification | CONFIDENTIAL — Internal Use Only; the Board extract may carry a wider classification decided by the CEO |
| After the meeting | **Section 9 completed within 2 working days** and lodged in the decision log |
| Language | English (`CG-010` §3.2 — executive and PMO material). Any figure quoted onward to staff is re-expressed through `CM-001` before it leaves the committee |
