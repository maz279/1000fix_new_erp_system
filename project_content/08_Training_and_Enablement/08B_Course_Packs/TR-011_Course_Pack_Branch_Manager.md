## 4. Facilitator Guide

| Module | Talking points | Common misconceptions | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 51+ managers reading the same number must read it the same way. **A metric you define locally is a metric nobody can compare** | *"My branch counts it differently because we're different."* | 90 |
| 3 | Cash controls are not distrust; they are what protects the cashier from suspicion when a shortfall occurs | *"My team is honest, we don't need the count."* | 120 |
| 6 | An override is designed, permitted and logged. An undocumented override is indistinguishable from a breach | *"I approved it verbally."* — `BR-STD-06` | 60 |
| 8 | **The safety duty is the branch manager's.** Establishments of 50+ workers require a safety committee, and training currency is a legal obligation, not an HR administrative task | *"HSE handles safety."* | 90 |
| 9 | A lapsed certification means a technician cannot be assigned to that brand's work — **the system will block it and the customer will still be waiting** | *"We'll sort the certificate later."* — `AS-10`, `AS-11` | 60 |
| 11 | If three branches show the same problem it is a system or process problem. If one does, look closer at that branch | *"Every problem is a people problem."* | 60 |

## 6. Exercises

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Interpret a branch dashboard and identify the three issues | `P06` sandbox | Branch with seeded problems | All three found and correctly categorised |

| 3 | Stock adjustment above threshold | Sandbox | Adjustment scenario | Approval sought; correct reason code |
| 4 | Inter-branch transfer | Sandbox | Transfer scenario | Source approval and destination confirmation both honoured |
| 5 | SLA risk board | Sandbox | 4 jobs near breach | Correct prioritisation and escalation |
| 6 | **Override scenario** | Sandbox | Request to bypass a designed control | Refuses the undesigned override; logs the designed one |
| 7 | Complaint role play | Pairs | Escalated customer | Rubric pass; within compensation limits |

| Level | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Reaction** | Confidence across the six accountabilities | Diagnostic only (`LS-02`) |
| **Learning** | Assessment and safety-walk pass | > 85% first attempt |
| **Behaviour** | Cash reconciliation completeness · override logging rate · certification lapses at the branch · safety records currency · metric-definition consistency across branches | **Lapses zero** (`KPI-062`); overrides 100% logged |
