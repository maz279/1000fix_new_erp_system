## 4. Facilitator Guide

| Module | Talking points | Common misconceptions |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Three screen switches is a **design** target the console is built for. If you need five, say so — that is product feedback, not personal failure | *"I'll just open everything at the start."* |
| 4 | Only approved, current articles may be sent to a customer (`BRU-KMS-11`). **Internal technical content is not customer content** | *"I'll paste the technician's note."* |
| 6 | 100% disposition coding is what makes the whole contact-centre dataset exist. **An uncoded contact never happened** | *"It was a quick one."* |
| 8 | Compensation limits are authority, not negotiation. Exceeding them without approval creates precedent the company must honour | *"It was easier to just agree."* |
| 9 | On a known defect you say **only** the approved message (`KW-07`). Speculating about a manufacturer's product is a commercial and legal exposure | *"I'll be honest with them about the model."* |
| 11 | A safety-relevant contact — a device that has overheated, a customer describing burning — is escalated immediately, not queued | *"I'll note it on the ticket."* |

## 10. Evaluation

| Level | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Reaction** | Confidence handling a complaint and a defect enquiry | Diagnostic only (`LS-02`) |
| **Learning** | Assessment, timed enquiry and coding pass | > 85% first attempt |
| **Behaviour** | **Screen switches per contact** · **disposition completeness** · first-contact resolution · defect-message compliance · escalation quality | Switches < 3; coding 100% |
| **Result** | **First-contact resolution above 78%** · average handling time falling 25% · **`KPI-025` self-service deflection 60% then 80%** · `KPI-026` AI first-contact resolution | Per `04-00` `P10`, `06-02` |

**Required Drivers** (`LS-04`): reinforce — three-screen path cards at the desk · encourage —
supervisor side-by-side for the first week · monitor — quality monitoring against the rubric,
weekly · reward — competence record; quality scores visible.

> **A caution on the Level 4 measures.** `KPI-025` deflection and `KPI-026` AI resolution both
> **reduce** contact volume. An agent may reasonably read them as measuring their own redundancy.
> They are not: deflection removes the low-value repeat enquiries and leaves the contacts that need
> a person. That framing belongs in module 1.
