## 4. Facilitator Guide

| Module | Talking points | Common misconceptions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Two officers reaching different decisions on the same facts is a contractual exposure**, not an inconsistency to be tidied up later | *"I used my judgement."* |
| 3 | `BRU-LMS-02` is a hard block. A seller active in a category they are not qualified for is a customer-safety and liability problem | *"They're experienced, the qualification is a formality."* |
| 4 | Every governance action is an act under the seller agreement. **The agreement, not our preference, sets what is permitted** | *"We can remove them, it's our platform."* |
| 6 | You are governing a relationship between two parties, one of whom is not our employee. Neutrality and record-keeping are the whole method | *"The customer is always right."* |
| 8 | Onboarding completion below 60% is usually lost at document verification, not at interest | *"Sellers aren't serious."* |
