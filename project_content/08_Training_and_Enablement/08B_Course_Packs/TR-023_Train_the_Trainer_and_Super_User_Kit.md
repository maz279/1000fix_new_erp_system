# Course Pack — Train-the-Trainer and Super User Kit

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Audience role(s) | **Champions and super-users drawn from existing roles** — typically `R08`, `R09`, `R10`, `R18`, `R27`, `R34` |
| Headcount | **~60–90** — target one to two champions per branch plus function champions |
| Duration | **16 hours** over four days, plus ongoing community |
| Delivery mode | Central or regional classroom; the only genuinely centralised delivery in the programme |
| Language | **Bengali primary** — champions deliver in Bengali |
| Prerequisites | **Their own role pack, passed** · nominated by their branch manager |
| Certification outcome | Internal champion certification, recorded in `MD-011` |
| Wave | W1 |

> **`PK-12`: the champion is not a trainer with a different title.** A trainer delivers a course
> and leaves. A champion works at the site, is asked questions at the counter three weeks later, and
> is the only person present when the system does something unexpected on a Tuesday afternoon.

## 4. Facilitator Guide

| Module | Talking points | Common misconceptions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Champions are chosen for **credibility and availability**, not for being the fastest user. The person colleagues already ask is the right person | *"Pick the best performer."* |
| 2 | Show, then let them do, then watch. **Explaining is the least effective of the three** and the most tempting | *"I'll just talk them through it."* |
| 5 | **"I don't know, I'll find out and come back" is a correct answer** and builds more trust than a confident guess. A wrong answer from a champion propagates across a site | *"I should know everything."* |
| 6 | Taking the keyboard finishes the task and teaches nothing. Sit on your hands | *"It's faster if I do it."* |
| 7 | Every time a champion answers from memory instead of pointing to the aid, the aid dies a little | *"It's quicker to just tell them."* |
| 8 | A question asked five times at one site is a content gap. **Asked at five sites, it is a product defect** | *"They just need more training."* |
| 10 | Champions hear everything. The role is to route what is actionable, not to carry the site's frustration | *"I'm the one who has to fix this."* |

## 6. Exercises

| # | Exercise | Method | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deliver a 20-minute module segment | Peer cohort | Structure, timing, clarity per rubric |
| 2 | **Run a sandbox exercise that breaks mid-way** | Practical, seeded failure | Recovers; cohort keeps learning |
| 3 | Answer eight questions, two of which they cannot know | Role play | **Both unknowns handled correctly** |
| 4 | Coach a slow learner through a transaction | Practical, observed | **Never takes the keyboard** |
| 5 | Question that has an aid | Role play | Points to the aid |
| 6 | Classify six questions | Scenario | Training / content / defect correct on all six |
| 7 | Escalate a system problem | Practical | Report is actionable without follow-up |
| 8 | **Assessed delivery — one full module** | Practical, observed by L&D | Rubric pass |

## 7. Assessment

| Item | Weight | Pass mark | Attempts | Re-certification |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Assessed delivery (exercise 8)** | 40% | Rubric pass | 3, with coaching between | **Before each wave they deliver** |
| Coaching and answering (3, 4, 5) | 25% | All passed | 3 | 24 months |
| Sandbox recovery (2) | 15% | Passed | 3 | 24 months |
| Gap classification and escalation (6, 7) | 20% | Both passed | 3 | 24 months |
| **Prerequisite: their own role pack passed** | Gate | — | — | Per that pack |

> **`PK-13`: a champion who has not passed their own role pack cannot be certified as a champion.**
> Credibility at the site depends on it, and a champion teaching a task they have not been assessed
> on propagates their own misunderstandings.

## 10. Evaluation

| Level | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Reaction** | Champion confidence delivering and being asked | Diagnostic only (`LS-02`) |
| **Learning** | Assessed delivery pass rate | > 80% first attempt |
| **Behaviour** | Sessions delivered per champion · questions routed vs answered from memory · gap reports raised · **champion retention in the role** | Retention above 70% per year |
| **Result** | **Delivery capacity achieved without central trainers** · time-to-competence maintained across waves · help-desk contacts per user falling after each wave | Per `RP-001` |

**Required Drivers** (`LS-04`): reinforce — the kit itself · encourage — **monthly community; the
champion network is its own encouragement mechanism** · monitor — sessions delivered, gaps raised,
retention · reward — champion certification in `MD-011`; recognition by the branch manager;
**recognised time allocation, not unpaid extra work**.

> **The retention measure is the one that will decide whether this works.** A champion network built
> on goodwill and no time allocation decays within two waves — the people who volunteered are the
> busiest people at the site. `O-02` records this as the central risk.
