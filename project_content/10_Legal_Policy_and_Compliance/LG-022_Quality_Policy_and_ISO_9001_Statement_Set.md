## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **QMS** | The quality management system — the policy, processes, controls, records and reviews taken together |
| **Scope** | What the certificate covers: which activities, at which sites (`QP1`, `QP2`) |
| **Quality objective** | A measurable target derived from the policy (ISO 9001:2015 clause 6.2) |
| **Nonconformity** | Work, output or a process not meeting a requirement |
| **Nonconforming output** | Output that must be controlled and dispositioned (clause 8.7) |
| **Root cause** | The underlying cause, **explicitly distinguished from a symptom** (`BRU-QMS-04`) |
| **CAPA** | Corrective and preventive action, closed only on **passed effectiveness verification** (`BRU-QMS-05`) |
| **Major non-conformance** | A failure requiring **containment within the declared interval and an 8D investigation** (`BRU-QMS-14`) |
| **Management review** | The clause 9.3 review, which **cannot be convened without every mandated input** (`BRU-QMS-08`) |
| **Surveillance audit** | The certification body's periodic audit between recertifications |

### 4.2 The Quality Policy

> **`CL-01`. This is the policy the certificate is issued against.** ISO 9001:2015 clause 5.2 requires
> it to be appropriate to the organisation, to include a commitment to satisfying applicable
> requirements and to continual improvement, to be available, and to be **communicated, understood and
> applied within the organisation.** Clause 5.2.2's *understood* is why §4.9 exists and why the
> Bengali version is not optional.

**1000FiX Services Limited — Quality Policy**

| # | Commitment |
|---|---|
| **QP-1** | **We repair well the first time.** Our purpose is a device returned working, to a customer who understands what was done and what it cost |
| **QP-2** | **We do what we said we would do, when we said we would do it.** A commitment we cannot measure is a commitment we will not keep, so we measure the ones we make |
| **QP-3** | **We tell the customer the truth**, including when the news is unwelcome — a fault we cannot fix, a delay, a price that has changed, a mistake we made |
| **QP-4** | **We meet the applicable statutory, regulatory and customer requirements** in everything we do, and we do not treat compliance as separate from quality |
| **QP-5** | **We hold our own work, our marketplace sellers' work and our suppliers' work to the same standard** (`BRU-QMS-17`). One standard, whoever does the work |
| **QP-6** | **We find the cause, not the culprit.** A nonconformity is information about a process. We record root cause, distinguish it from symptom, and fix the process (`BRU-QMS-04`) |
| **QP-7** | **We verify that a fix worked** before we call it closed (`BRU-QMS-05`) |
| **QP-8** | **We do not close a customer's complaint until the customer says it is closed** (`BRU-QMS-04` in `05-05`, `BRU-CXM-04`) |
| **QP-9** | **We publish no quality figure without its sample size and its method** (`BRU-QMS-20`) |

| **5.2** | **A certification claim the certificate does not support is a misrepresentation**, with tender, contractual and reputational consequence (`CC-01`, `CC-02`) |
| **5.3** | **A quality figure published without its sample size and method is misleading**, which is why `BRU-QMS-20` prohibits it and `QP-9` states it as policy |
| **5.4** | **A certificate lapse is an operating exposure**, not an administrative one: **a licence, registration or certification approaching expiry escalates on its declared lead time, and a lapse generates a mandatory incident record** (`BRU-GRC-22`) |
| **5.5** | The quality policy is a commitment to a standard of work; **it is not a contractual warranty**, and `LG-013` is where warranty obligations live |
| **5.6** | Nothing limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited |

## 7. Termination and Consequences

| Event | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **A checklist is incomplete** | **The order cannot reach Ready** (`BRU-QMS-01`) |
| **A major non-conformance is raised** | Containment within the declared interval, then an 8D investigation (`BRU-QMS-14`) |
| **A CAPA is overdue** | Escalates on the declared schedule; **an extension requires approval and a reason** (`BRU-QMS-19`) |
| **The same root cause recurs** | Severity escalates automatically and it is tabled at management review (`BRU-QMS-09`) |
| **A systemic exposure is identified** | Escalated to `M60`, which must accept or decline explicitly (`BRU-GRC-21`) |
| **A certificate lapses or is suspended** | **A mandatory incident record** (`BRU-GRC-22`); **every claim in every document must be withdrawn** (`CC-01`, `LG-018` `CM-R6` sweep principle) |
| **A management-review input is missing** | **The review is postponed** (`MR-R1`) |
| **This policy set is superseded** | Prior versions retained; **records retained three certification cycles** (`BRU-QMS-13`, `LG-009` `DC-19`) |
