# LG-006 — Vendor Portal Terms and Supplier Code of Conduct

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Document class | **Terms + Code** — portal terms in §4, a binding Code of Conduct in §5 |
| Counterparty | A parts supplier, courier partner, sub-contractor or recycling partner using `P05` |
| Governing law | **Bangladesh** |
| Effective date | On publication of version 1.0 — *pending approval* |
| Acceptance capture method | **`AC-2` e-signature at qualification.** `BRU-SRM2-01` makes activation for a category the gate: a supplier that is not Active for the category cannot receive a purchase order |
| Version accepted by users | Recorded in `LG-001` per supplier entity and per named user |
| External counsel review | **Required** — the §5 Code's enforceability as a contractual term and the §5.4 anti-bribery standard |
| Bengali version | **Not required** (`CG-010` §3.2, §3.3 — vendors and suppliers contract in English). **See `O-03`, which is a real objection for small recyclers and courier subcontractors** |

> ⚠ **Class B, and the file with the widest counterparty spread in the category.** A multinational
> parts distributor and a two-person e-waste collector both accept this document. §4 is uniform;
> §5 is where proportionality lives, and §5.7 says so explicitly rather than leaving a small
> counterparty to guess which standards apply to it.
>
> **Conformance note.** All ten template sections retained in order. The Code of Conduct occupies
> §5 alongside liability because the register allocates one file to both and `CP-01` forbids
> splitting it into an unregistered second. **§5 is therefore longer than the template's §5
> normally is, and its liability content is §5.8–§5.12.**

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Qualification** | Approval to supply a category, granted **per category and per legal entity, never globally** (`BRU-SRM2-03`) |
| **Active** | The status that permits a purchase order to be raised. **The gate is evaluated by `M42` and enforced by `M16`** (`BRU-SRM2-01`) |
| **Provisional activation** | A time-boxed activation that **blocks automatically at expiry with no grace period** (`BRU-SRM2-12`) |
| **Scorecard** | The published performance record — on-time delivery, quality, responsiveness. **Immutable once published; a correction is a re-publication with a stated reason** (`BRU-SRM2-09`) |
| **Consignment terms** | Terms recorded in `M42`, which are **the authority for inventory enforcement**; `M15` holds no independent terms (`BRU-SRM2-15`) |
| **Code** | The Supplier Code of Conduct in §5, a binding term of this agreement (subject to `VE1`) |
| **Downstream destination** | Where a recycling partner sends material after it leaves them, **declared at qualification and verified at least annually** (`BRU-ESG-18`) |
| **Governing text** | This English text — subject to `O-03` |

## 4. Terms

### 4.1 Portal access

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **4.1.1** | Access is granted for as long as the Supplier is qualified, by named individual |
| **4.1.2** | **The Supplier sees only its own records** — purchase orders, receipts, invoices, its own scorecard — with no cost, margin or other party's data (`BRU-SRM2-20`, `EA-01`, `EA-02`, `EA-06`) |
| **4.1.3** | A courier partner sees only consignments assigned to it, **with the minimum data needed to deliver** (`EA-07`) |
| **4.1.4** | Sessions time out after 30 minutes (`EA-08`); MFA is mandatory for any action with financial consequence (`EA-09`) |
| **4.1.5** | The Supplier notifies the Company promptly when a Portal User leaves |

### 4.2 Qualification and status

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **4.2.1** | Qualification is **per category and per legal entity** (`BRU-SRM2-03`). A category the Supplier is not qualified for is not open to it |
| **4.2.2** | **A purchase order may only be raised to a Supplier that is Active for the relevant category on the order date** (`BRU-SRM2-01`). The Supplier should not accept or act on an instruction outside that |
| **4.2.3** | **A qualification or mandatory document that expires automatically suspends the Supplier for the affected categories** (`BRU-SRM2-04`). The portal warns in advance; the suspension is not discretionary |
| **4.2.4** | **A Supplier may not be qualified to supply genuine-OEM parts without current documentary evidence of authorisation or authorised-distribution status** (`BRU-SRM2-05`). The Supplier keeps that evidence current |
| **4.2.5** | The Supplier is screened for sanctions and for related-party conflicts. **A confirmed sanctions match or a confirmed counterfeit supply blocks the Supplier permanently, by CFO or CEO authority, and cannot be reversed below that authority** (`BRU-SRM2-16`) — `O-05` |
| **4.2.6** | **A related-party or conflict-of-interest match must be declared and approved before activation** (`BRU-SRM2-17`). The Supplier declares any relationship between its owners or officers and a Company employee |
| **4.2.7** | Provisional activation is time-boxed and **blocks automatically at expiry** (`BRU-SRM2-12`) |
| **4.2.8** | The Supplier is risk-rated, and due diligence is proportionate to the rating, with periodic reassessment (`FR-GRC-042`) — `O-04` |

### 4.3 Ordering, delivery and returns

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **4.3.1** | RFQ responses state price, lead time and validity. A quotation binds the Supplier for its stated validity |
| **4.3.2** | A purchase order is acknowledged or queried through the portal. **Acknowledgement is the Supplier's confirmation of price, quantity and date** |
| **4.3.3** | **The Company may not place an order above the approved price list without a price change approved in `M42`** (`BRU-SRM2-14`). A Supplier receiving such an order should query it |
| **4.3.4** | Goods are inspected on receipt; the outcome is visible in the portal (`DT-024`). Rejected goods follow the return-to-vendor route with a debit advice (`DT-025`) |
| **4.3.5** | **Counterfeit or misrepresented goods are a fundamental breach**, not a quality exception (§4.2.5) |
| **4.3.6** | Consignment stock remains the Supplier's until consumed, on the terms recorded in `M42` (`BRU-SRM2-15`) |

### 4.4 Invoicing and payment

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **4.4.1** | Invoices are submitted against a purchase order through the portal and are matched before payment |
| **4.4.2** | **A valid Mushak 6.3 tax invoice from an active BIN is required** for the Company to claim input VAT (`BRU-TAX-06`, `NFR-101`). An invoice that does not comply cannot be paid as compliant |
| **4.4.3** | **A change to bank details requires documented verification and second-person approval, is retained immutably, and generates a security event** (`BRU-SRM2-07`). The Company will independently verify a change through a channel the Supplier did not supply in the request — `VE6` |
| **4.4.4** | Tax is deducted at source where required, at the determined rate, and a certificate is issued within the statutory window (`BRU-TAX-07`, `BRU-TAX-10`). **Where the Supplier cannot produce a valid proof of return submission, or payment is not by bank transfer, the elevated rate applies automatically** (`BRU-TAX-08`) |
| **4.4.5** | The Company's payment schedule and remittance advice are visible in the portal |
| **4.4.6** | The user who creates or qualifies a Supplier may not approve that Supplier's invoice (`BRU-SRM2-06`, `SOD-01`) |

### 4.5 Performance

The Supplier's scorecard is published in the portal. **Performance facts originate in the Company's
receipt and warehouse records and are not editable** (`BRU-SRM2-08`). **A Supplier scoring in the
lowest band for two consecutive periods enters mandatory corrective action; a third consecutive
period triggers suspension review** (`BRU-SRM2-10`). Corrective actions overdue beyond their due
date escalate (`BRU-SRM2-11`). A published scorecard is immutable; a correction is a re-publication
with a stated reason (`BRU-SRM2-09`).

### 4.6 Audit and inspection

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **4.6.1** | The Company may audit the Supplier's compliance with these terms and the §5 Code, on reasonable notice, during business hours |
| **4.6.2** | **Suppliers are audited against the same standards as owned operations** (`BRU-QMS-17`) |
| **4.6.3** | The Company may extend an audit to a **sub-tier supplier or downstream destination** where the Code or a client obligation requires it — `VE3`, `O-06` |
| **4.6.4** | The Company may share audit findings with a corporate or brand client where its contract with that client requires supply-chain assurance, **limited to findings and excluding the Supplier's commercially confidential information** |
| **4.6.5** | An audit does not transfer responsibility. **A Supplier's compliance is the Supplier's own** |

### 4.7 Recycling partners — additional terms

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **4.7.1** | The recycling partner holds and maintains **current** authorisation for the material it handles. **A transfer to a partner whose certification has expired is blocked at the moment of transfer** (`BRU-ESG-02`) — the block is systemic, not a reminder |
| **4.7.2** | Every batch transfer requires a complete manifest and, where applicable, a hazardous-material declaration (`BRU-ESG-03`) |
| **4.7.3** | **Every item in a transferred batch must be reconciled to a returned disposal certificate. Unreconciled items remain open exceptions** (`BRU-ESG-04`) and are the partner's to close |
| **4.7.4** | **Downstream destinations are declared at qualification and verified at least annually** (`BRU-ESG-18`). An undeclared destination is a breach |
| **4.7.5** | **A transfer whose destination is outside Bangladesh requires a recorded Basel prior-informed-consent reference before execution** (`BRU-ESG-06`) |
| **4.7.6** | The partner reports material recovery against the batch that produced it (`BRU-ESG-19`) |
| **4.7.7** | **No device reaches the partner without a certified wipe or physical-destruction record** (`BRU-ESG-01`, `LG-017`). The partner must not accept one that arrives without it, and must report it |
| **4.7.8** | The environmental claims the Company may make about the partner's outcomes are governed by `LG-018` — **`O-02`** |

### 4.8 Courier and logistics partners — additional terms

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **4.8.1** | Custody transfers on recorded handover with GPS, timestamp and both parties' identity (`P09` capability set, `DT-028`) |
| **4.8.2** | The partner's staff receive **only the minimum data needed to deliver** (`EA-07`) and must not retain or reuse it |
| **4.8.3** | Where the partner collects payment, it is remitted on the agreed cycle and is the Company's money from the moment of collection |
| **4.8.4** | An exception — customer absent, address wrong, refused, damaged — is recorded at the time, not reconstructed later |
| **4.8.5** | A customer device is high-value property in someone else's custody. **Loss or damage in transit is the partner's, subject to the framework agreement's liability terms** |

### 4.9 Confidentiality and acceptable use

Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential. **The Company's pricing, cost,
volumes and customer data are confidential.** The Supplier must not attempt unauthorised access,
probe the platform's security, or extract data in bulk beyond what the portal provides
(`EA-11`, `EA-12`).

## 5. Supplier Code of Conduct, Liability, Exclusions and Limits

> **§5.1–§5.7 are the Code. §5.8–§5.12 are liability.** Both are in §5 because the register
> allocates one file (conformance note).

### 5.1 The standard the Code sets

The Company requires every Supplier to meet the standards below **as a term of doing business**,
not as an aspiration (subject to `VE1`). Where local law sets a higher standard, local law applies.

### 5.2 Labour and human rights

| # | Standard |
|---|---|
| **CD-01** | **No child labour.** No person below the minimum age set by the Bangladesh Labour Act 2006 is employed on work for the Company |
| **CD-02** | **No forced, bonded, trafficked or involuntary labour.** No retention of identity documents, no recruitment fees charged to workers, no coerced overtime |
| **CD-03** | **Wages** meet or exceed the applicable legal minimum, are paid on time, in full, and are documented |
| **CD-04** | **Working hours and rest** comply with the Labour Act and Labour Rules 2015 |
| **CD-05** | **No discrimination** on grounds of sex, religion, ethnicity, disability, pregnancy or union membership |
| **CD-06** | **No harassment or abuse**, including physical, verbal, sexual or psychological |
| **CD-07** | **Freedom of association** and the right to collective bargaining are respected where law provides for them |

### 5.3 Health, safety and environment

| # | Standard |
|---|---|
| **CD-08** | A safe workplace with assessed risks, controls, protective equipment and training |
| **CD-09** | Hazardous materials handled, stored and transported per their specific rules **without exception** (`BRU-ESG-04`, `BRU-ESG-07`, `BRU-ESG-08`) |
| **CD-10** | Incidents recorded, investigated and reported; a serious incident involving Company work is reported to the Company |
| **CD-11** | Environmental permits held and current; waste routed only to authorised destinations (§4.7) |
| **CD-12** | Emissions, effluent and waste managed to the applicable standard under the Environment Conservation Act 1995 and its rules |

### 5.4 Business integrity

| # | Standard |
|---|---|
| **CD-13** | **No bribery.** No payment, gift, hospitality or advantage offered or accepted to obtain or retain business or to influence a decision, including a facilitation payment |
| **CD-14** | **No kickback** to a Company employee, and no undisclosed benefit of any kind. Gift and hospitality limits are in `LG-023` |
| **CD-15** | **Conflicts of interest declared** before activation and whenever they arise (`BRU-SRM2-17`) |
| **CD-16** | **No anti-competitive conduct** — no bid rigging, no price fixing, no market sharing in Company tenders |
| **CD-17** | **No money laundering, no sanctions breach, no dealing in the proceeds of crime** (Money Laundering Prevention Act 2012) |
| **CD-18** | **Accurate records.** No false invoice, false certificate, false test result or falsified document |
| **CD-19** | **Genuine goods.** No counterfeit, grey-market-misrepresented, stolen or unlawfully sourced items (§4.2.5, `BRU-MKP-11` for the seller analogue) |

### 5.5 Speaking up

The Supplier, and **its workers**, may report a suspected breach of this Code through the Company's
confidential channel in
[`LG-023_Anti_Bribery_Ethics_and_Whistleblowing_Content.md`](LG-023_Anti_Bribery_Ethics_and_Whistleblowing_Content.md).
**The Company will not penalise a Supplier for a report made in good faith**, and cases are handled
under restricted access with anonymised outcome reporting (`FR-GRC-044`, `BRU-GRC-20`) — subject to
`VE7`.

### 5.6 Data and confidentiality

The Supplier processes personal data it receives from the Company **only as the Company's processor,
only for the purpose supplied, and under equivalent obligations that flow down by this clause**
(`NFR-098`, `BRU-GRC-18`). §6 sets the detail.

### 5.7 Proportionality and flow-down — **pending `VE2`, `O-04`, `O-06`**

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **5.7.1** | Every Supplier must meet §5.2, §5.3 and §5.4 in full. **Those are not tiered** |
| **5.7.2** | The **evidence** the Company requires is proportionate to the Supplier's risk rating: self-declaration at the lowest tier, documentary evidence in the middle, on-site audit at the highest — `O-04` |
| **5.7.3** | A small Supplier is not excused a standard because of its size. It may be given a shorter route to evidencing it |
| **5.7.4** | The Supplier requires equivalent standards of **its own** suppliers and subcontractors used for Company work — `O-06` |
| **5.7.5** | A Code breach is dealt with by corrective action where the Supplier engages with it, and by suspension or removal where it does not, or where the breach is in §5.2 `CD-01`, `CD-02` or §5.4 `CD-13`, `CD-17`, `CD-19` |

### 5.8 Liability — general

Each party is liable for loss it causes by breach. The Supplier is liable for goods and services it
supplies, and for the acts of its staff and subcontractors on Company work or premises.

### 5.9 Indemnity

The Supplier indemnifies the Company against claims, penalties and losses arising from the
Supplier's breach of §4.2.4, §4.2.5, §4.7, §5.2, §5.3 or §5.4 — provisional pending `VE4`.

### 5.10 Exclusions

The Company is not liable to the Supplier for: the volume or continuity of orders; a qualification
or activation decision properly made under §4.2; a scorecard result computed from Company records
(`BRU-SRM2-08`); or a suspension arising from the Supplier's own document lapse (`BRU-SRM2-04`).

### 5.11 Cap

Where no framework agreement sets a cap, the Company's aggregate liability under these terms is
limited to the value of purchase orders placed with the Supplier in the twelve months preceding the
claim — provisional pending G5.

### 5.12 Non-excludable

Nothing limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited, including for fraud, for death or personal
injury caused by negligence, or for a statutory data-protection liability.

## 6. Data Protection

| **6.8** | Supplier records are retained per `LG-009` — **not less than the tax retention on the transactions they evidence**, and longer where a dispute or hold exists |
| **6.9** | Bank-account details are restricted (`S3`) data, encrypted and masked except the last four digits (`05-02` §10) |

## 7. Termination and Consequences

| Event | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Document or qualification lapse** | **Automatic suspension for the affected categories** (`BRU-SRM2-04`); reinstated on renewal |
| **Provisional activation expiry** | **Automatic block, no grace period** (`BRU-SRM2-12`) |
| **Two consecutive lowest-band periods** | Mandatory corrective action; a third triggers suspension review (`BRU-SRM2-10`) |
| **Confirmed sanctions match or counterfeit supply** | **Permanent block by CFO or CEO authority; irreversible below that authority** (`BRU-SRM2-16`) |
| **Code breach in `CD-01`, `CD-02`, `CD-13`, `CD-17` or `CD-19`** | Suspension pending investigation, then removal unless the breach is disproved (§5.7.5) |

| **In every case** | Open purchase orders are settled or cancelled per their terms; consignment stock is reconciled and returned; §4.9, §5.6, §6 and the retention position survive; **an unreconciled recycling batch remains an open exception until closed** (`BRU-ESG-04`) |
