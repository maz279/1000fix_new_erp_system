> ⚠ **Class A — statutory. Two distinct exposures, and they fail in opposite directions.**
>
> **The first is regulatory.** The e-waste rules introduced **extended producer responsibility for
> the first time in Bangladesh**, with **registration, waste-management plans and rising collection
> targets — 10% in the first year of implementation, then 20%, 30%, 40% and 50%**. Whether the
> company is a regulated entity, and against which target year, is **not established** (`EW1`).
>
> **The second is reputational and legal in a different way.** An environmental claim is a
> representation. *"We recycle responsibly"* is a statement the company must be able to substantiate
> item by item — and `BRU-ESG-11` already forbids a manual entry path for a published figure, which
> is the strongest anti-greenwashing control in the register.
>
> **§4.6 is therefore a permitted-claims list with the evidence required for each.**
>
> **Conformance note.** All ten template sections retained in order. The regulatory position is §4.2,
> declarations §4.5, permitted claims §4.6, within §4 Terms. Gate questions and open items are §1.2
> and §1.3 (`CP-09`).

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **E-waste** | Electrical and electronic equipment, and its components, at end of life |
| **EPR — extended producer responsibility** | The principle that a producer bears responsibility for a product at end of life. **Introduced in Bangladesh for the first time by the 2021 rules** ‡ |
| **Collection target** | The proportion of waste equipment a regulated entity must collect, **rising 10% / 20% / 30% / 40% / 50% by implementation year** ‡ — `EW2`, `EW3` |
| **Batch** | A grouped transfer of items to a disposal or recovery route |
| **Manifest** | The item-level record accompanying a batch (`BRU-ESG-03`) |
| **Disposal certificate** | The recycler's evidence that specific items were processed — `DT-035`. **Every item must reconcile to one** (`BRU-ESG-04`) |
| **Chain of custody** | The unbroken record from collection to certified disposal. **A gap is an exception requiring investigation** (`BRU-ESG-10`) |
| **Downstream destination** | Where material goes after the company's partner. **Declared at qualification, verified at least annually** (`BRU-ESG-18`) |
| **Triage** | The decision on an item's route — reuse, refurbish, harvest, recycle. **Recorded with assessor, criteria and evidence, and immutable once made** (`BRU-ESG-09`) |
| **Emissions factor** | The coefficient converting an activity into an environmental figure. **Its version is recorded with every published figure** (`BRU-ESG-12`) |
| **‡** | A position resting on a secondary source, unverified, **pending G6** |

### 4.6 Environmental claims — permitted, prohibited, and the evidence each needs

> **`CL-03`. This is the anti-greenwashing control, and `BRU-ESG-11` is what makes it enforceable:**
> *"Published environmental figures are computed from posted transactions; no manual entry path
> exists."* A figure that cannot be computed **cannot be published**, because there is no way to type
> it in.
>
> **`BRU-ESG-12`** adds the second half: *"Every published impact figure records the emissions-factor
> version used"* — **and `O-05` records that no factor set exists**, so **no quantified claim in the
> table below can be made yet.**

**Permitted, with the evidence required:**

| # | Claim | Evidence required | Status |
|---|---|---|---|

| **CM-2** | *"N units were transferred to authorised recyclers"* | Manifests plus partner authorisation current at transfer (`BRU-ESG-02`) | Available |
| **CM-3** | *"N units reconciled to disposal certificates"* | Returned certificates, item by item (`BRU-ESG-04`) | Available |

| **CM-5** | *"N kg of material was recovered"* | Partner recovery reports reconciled to batch weight (`BRU-ESG-05`, `BRU-ESG-19`) | Available |
| **CM-6** | *"We refurbished and returned N devices to use"* | `M14` posted transactions | Available |
