## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bribe** | Any financial or other advantage offered, given, requested or accepted to induce or reward improper performance, or to influence a decision |
| **Facilitation payment** | A small payment to secure or expedite a routine action a person is already obliged to perform. **Treated as a bribe** (§4.2.4) |
| **Kickback** | A benefit returned to a person who awarded or influenced business |
| **Public official** | Any government, regulatory, judicial, municipal, military or state-enterprise officer, and any person acting for one |
| **Conflict of interest** | A personal, family or financial interest that could influence, or appear to influence, a decision taken for the company |
| **Related party** | A person or entity connected to a company decision-maker — the scope is `EB6` |
| **Concern** | A report that something improper has happened, is happening or may happen |
| **Reporter** | The person raising a concern, whether named or anonymous |
| **Good faith** | An honest belief that the concern is substantially true. **It does not require the reporter to be right** |
| **Retaliation** | Any detriment to a reporter or a witness because of a report |
| **`T5`** | The restricted isolation tier — allow-list access, step-up authentication, every access logged (`BRU-GRC-20`) |

## 4. Terms

### 4.1 The standard

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **4.1.1** | **The company does not pay bribes and does not accept them.** There is no threshold, no local exception and no commercial justification |
| **4.1.2** | **A contract lost for refusing to pay a bribe is an acceptable outcome.** Anyone who loses one is supported, not questioned |
| **4.1.3** | **No employee will be penalised for a loss of business resulting from compliance with this policy.** That sentence exists because its absence is why policies fail |
| **4.1.4** | **The standard applies to anyone acting for the company** — employee, contractor, agent, seller, supplier, partner |
| **4.1.5** | **A person who is unsure asks before acting**, and asking is never held against them |

### 4.2 Prohibited conduct

| # | Prohibition |
|---|---|
| **EB-01** | **Offering, giving, promising, requesting or accepting a bribe**, directly or through anyone else |
| **EB-02** | **Offering anything of value to a public official** to obtain or retain business or influence a decision — §4.3 sets a zero-value rule |
| **EB-03** | **Making or accepting a kickback** on a purchase, a payout, a claim, a commission or a settlement |
| **EB-04** | **Making a facilitation payment.** A small payment to speed a routine action is a bribe — subject to `EB5` |
| **EB-05** | **Using a third party to do what this policy prohibits** — an agent, a consultant, a relative, a partner, a seller |
| **EB-06** | **Concealing a payment or misdescribing it** in any record. **A false invoice, a false expense claim or a miscoded payment is a separate breach** |
| **EB-07** | **Awarding business to a related party without declaration and approval** (`BRU-SRM2-17`) |
| **EB-08** | **Circumventing an approval limit or a duty separation**, including by splitting a transaction |
| **EB-09** | **Dealing with a sanctioned party**, or with a party after a confirmed counterfeit supply (`BRU-SRM2-16`) |
| **EB-10** | **Money laundering, or dealing in the proceeds of crime** |
| **EB-11** | **Anti-competitive conduct in a tender** — bid rigging, price fixing, cover pricing, market sharing |
| **EB-12** | **Retaliating against a reporter or a witness** (`LG-020` `AU-16`) |
| **EB-13** | **Obstructing an investigation**, destroying a record, or warning a subject |

> **`CL-01`. `EB-06` is where a bribery case is actually detected.** Bribes rarely appear as bribes;
> they appear as an unexplained consultancy fee, an inflated expense, a miscoded payment. `BRU-ARB-09`
> and `BRU-ARB-10` already require a credit note to have an approver other than the requester and a
> reason from a controlled taxonomy — **the accounting controls are the anti-bribery controls.**

### 4.3 Gifts and hospitality — **limits pending `EB4`, `O-05`**

| Situation | Rule | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| **Anything to or from a public official** | **Zero value. Nothing, ever** — subject to `EB4` | None available |
| A modest promotional item, both directions | Permitted | None; recorded if above a nominal value |
| Business hospitality — a meal at a business meeting | Permitted if proportionate, infrequent, and not near a tender decision | **Above the stated limit: line manager** — *value pending `O-05`* |
| Travel or accommodation paid by a counterparty | **Not permitted** unless approved in advance by the CFO | CFO |
| Cash, or a cash equivalent — voucher, gift card, credit | **Never**, in either direction | None available |
| A gift during a live tender, negotiation or claim decision | **Never** | None available |
| A gift to a decision-maker's family member | **Treated as a gift to the decision-maker** | — |
| A gift that cannot be refused without offence | **Accepted on the company's behalf, declared, and disposed of by the company** | Line manager, recorded |

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **GH-R1** | **Register it, do not judge it.** A gift or hospitality above the stated value is declared in the register whether or not it was accepted |
| **GH-R2** | **The test is not the value; it is the timing and the influence.** A modest meal during a tender is worse than a larger one outside it |
| **GH-R3** | **`O-05`: the values are not set**, so `GH-R1` currently has no threshold |
| **GH-R4** | The register is reviewed by the CFO periodically, and patterns are followed up |

| # | Clause |
|---|---|

> controls already exist in the platform, so a reader can see that the policy is enforced rather than
> merely stated. **An ethics policy that does not point at its controls reads as aspiration.**

### 4.6 Political and charitable giving — **pending `EB7`**

The company makes **no political donation**. A charitable donation requires approval at the level
`SP-004` sets, is made to an organisation with no connection to a live commercial decision, and is
recorded. **A charitable donation requested by a counterparty during a tender is refused.**

### 4.7 Speaking up — the channel and the protection

> **`CL-03`. This is the section that determines whether the file has any effect.**
>
> A person deciding whether to report weighs one thing: **what happens to me.** `FR-GRC-044` provides
> a confidential channel; `BRU-GRC-20` makes cases `T5`. **Neither tells a rider in Chattogram whether
> they will still have a job.**

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **4.7.1** | **Anyone may raise a concern** — employee, contractor, and a supplier's or seller's worker (`LG-006` §5.5, subject to `EB9`) |
| **4.7.2** | **A concern may be raised anonymously.** An anonymous concern is investigated on its content — subject to `EB2`, and §4.7.6 states the limit honestly |
| **4.7.3** | **Routes** — the line manager where that is comfortable; **the confidential channel where it is not**; Legal Counsel; the CFO; **and where the concern is about any of those people, the route bypasses them** |
| **4.7.4** | **The company undertakes that no person will suffer detriment for raising a concern in good faith.** That includes dismissal, disciplinary action, a changed role, a withheld promotion, exclusion, or any informal consequence |
| **4.7.5** | **`EB-12` makes retaliation a disciplinary offence in its own right**, investigated as a separate matter from the original concern |
| **4.7.6** | **The honest limit.** Until `EB1` is answered, §4.7.4 is a **policy undertaking by the company**, and **is not represented as a statutory whistleblower protection.** A reporter is entitled to know the difference, and this clause is where they are told (`O-02`) |
| **4.7.7** | **A reporter is told what happened**, so far as confidentiality and law permit. **A channel that never reports back is a channel that stops receiving** |
| **4.7.8** | **A reporter who turns out to be wrong is protected**, provided they acted in good faith. **A reporter who knowingly makes a false report is not** |
| **4.7.9** | **A person may report their own involvement**, and doing so is taken into account |
| **4.7.10** | **The channel is available in Bengali**, and a report may be made in Bengali and will be answered in Bengali |
| **4.7.11** | **`O-03`: the channel does not exist yet.** This policy is not published until it does |

### 4.8 How a concern is handled

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **4.8.1** | A concern is acknowledged, assessed and assigned to an investigator **independent of the subject and of the reporting line** |
| **4.8.2** | **Access to the case is restricted — `T5`: allow-list, step-up authentication, every access logged** (`BRU-GRC-20`). **A colleague cannot look up who reported what** (`LG-020` §6.7) |
| **4.8.3** | The subject is told of the allegation and given an opportunity to respond, **at the point that does not prejudice the investigation** |
| **4.8.4** | **The reporter's identity is disclosed only where necessary and lawful**, and the reporter is told before it happens where that is possible |
| **4.8.5** | Where an offence may have been committed, **Legal Counsel determines whether an external report is required** — `EB3` |
| **4.8.6** | Where the concern is a personal-data breach, `LG-008` §4.8 and `LG-021` `IS-10` apply in parallel |
| **4.8.7** | Where it indicates a process failure, a non-conformance is raised (`BRU-CXM-06`, `LG-022` §4.8) |
| **4.8.8** | **Anonymised outcomes are reported to the audit committee** (`FR-GRC-044`) — **`O-04`: there is no audit committee** |
| **4.8.9** | **A concern is never closed on the basis that the reporter withdrew it.** The company decides whether the issue is real |
| **4.8.10** | Records are retained per `LG-009` `RC-21`, and are `T5` throughout |

### 4.9 Consequences

| Conduct | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **A bribe offered, given, requested or accepted** | **Dismissal, and referral to the authorities** |
| **A facilitation payment made** | Investigated; dismissal unless a genuine safety-related exception applies — `EB5` |
| **A kickback** | Dismissal and referral; recovery pursued |
| **An undeclared conflict** | Disciplinary action up to dismissal, proportionate to the benefit and the concealment |
| **Concealing or misdescribing a payment** | Treated as seriously as the underlying conduct (`EB-06`) |
| **Circumventing an approval or duty separation** | Disciplinary action; the transaction is reversed where possible; reported under `FR-GRC-021` |
| **Retaliation against a reporter** | **A separate disciplinary offence**, up to dismissal (`EB-12`) |
| **Obstructing an investigation** | Dismissal |
| **A supplier, seller or partner breach** | Suspension, then removal (`LG-006` §5.7.5, `LG-004` `PH-07`); **a confirmed sanctions or counterfeit match is a permanent block reversible only by the CFO or CEO** (`BRU-SRM2-16`) |
| **A knowingly false report** | Disciplinary action (`4.7.8`) |

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **4.9.1** | **A consequence follows a process** — `LG-020` `AU4` and the Labour Act framework govern it |
| **4.9.2** | **`LG-020` `O-05` applies: no sanction ladder exists**, so §4.9 is a classification rather than a tariff |
| **4.9.3** | Letters are `DT-042`; **the letter is not the process** |

## 5. Liability, Exclusions and Limits

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **5.1** | This is an internal policy and creates no third-party rights, **except that §4.7.4's undertaking is intended to be relied on by a reporter** |

| **5.5** | **A reporter acting in good faith is protected by §4.7.4, subject to the honest limit in §4.7.6** |
| **5.6** | Nothing limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited |

## 6. Data Protection

> Must reconcile with `LG-008`, `LG-009`, `LG-012`. Check **F6**.

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **6.1** | A whistleblowing case contains personal data about the reporter, the subject and witnesses — `LG-008` `P-18`, on legitimate ground and legal obligation |
| **6.2** | **Cases are `T5`** — allow-list access, step-up authentication, every access logged (`BRU-GRC-20`) |
| **6.3** | **The subject of a report is a data subject too.** A subject-access request is handled under `LG-010`, and **`RF-06` refuses access that would prejudice a live investigation** — the extent is `EB8` |
| **6.4** | **The reporter's identity is minimised in the record** and is not stored where the general case narrative is readable |

## 7. Termination and Consequences

| Event | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **A concern is substantiated** | §4.9 consequence; a process fix through `LG-022` §4.8; an enterprise risk raised where systemic (`BRU-GRC-21`) |
| **A concern is not substantiated** | Recorded, the reporter told, **and the reporter remains protected** (`4.7.8`) |
| **A reporter leaves the company** | **Protection and confidentiality survive.** A reference must not reflect the report |
| **A subject leaves during an investigation** | The investigation continues; the outcome is recorded |
| **A supplier is removed** | `LG-006` §7; outstanding matters settled net of recovery |
| **A legal hold applies** | **Records are never purged** (`BRU-SEC-23`, `BRU-DMS-02`) |
| **This policy is superseded** | Prior versions retained permanently (`LG-009` `DC-23`), because a disciplinary decision is judged against the version in force |
