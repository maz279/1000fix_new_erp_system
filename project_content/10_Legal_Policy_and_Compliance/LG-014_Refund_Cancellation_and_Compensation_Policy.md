> ⚠ **Class B. The failure mode this file prevents is not a lawsuit; it is 51+ branches each
> inventing an answer.**
>
> A refund decision is made in front of an unhappy customer by an agent who wants the conversation
> to end. Without a published policy the agent's options are to refuse (and generate a complaint) or
> to concede (and generate an uncontrolled cost). **Both are expensive, and neither is a decision the
> company made.**
>
> **§4.7 is therefore an authority matrix, not guidance.** It says who may agree what, up to what
> amount, without asking — which is the only form of policy that actually changes behaviour at a
> counter.
>
> **Conformance note.** All ten template sections retained in order. The entitlement matrix is §4.3,
> the authority matrix §4.7, within §4 Terms. Gate questions and open items are §1.2 and §1.3
> (`CP-09`).

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cancellation** | The customer or the company ending a booked or in-progress service before completion |
| **Refund** | Returning money the customer paid |
| **Credit note** | The accounting instrument that reverses or reduces an invoice (`DT-015`). **A refund of an invoiced amount requires one** (`BRU-ARB-09`) |
| **Deduction** | An amount properly retained from a refund — work performed, parts consumed, a disclosed charge |
| **Compensation** | A payment or credit for loss or inconvenience the company caused, made under §4.8 |
| **Goodwill** | A discretionary gesture **without admission of liability**, under §4.9 |
| **Service credit** | A contractual SLA remedy. **Not a refund** — `LG-015` |
| **Original medium** | The payment method the customer used |
| **Governing text** | The **English** version (`LG-001` `LR-03`) |

## 4. Terms

### 4.1 The principles

| # | Principle |
|---|---|
| **RP-01** | **The customer is not charged for work they did not approve or the company did not do** |
| **RP-02** | **The customer is not refunded for work they approved and the company did** — unless the work was defective, in which case `LG-013` applies first |
| **RP-03** | **A refund is not the standard answer to a service failure.** Re-performance usually serves the customer better and costs the company less; a refund is right where re-performance no longer helps |
| **RP-04** | **Every decision has a reason code from a controlled taxonomy** (`BRU-ARB-10`, §4.10) |
| **RP-05** | **Nobody approves their own refund, credit note or goodwill payment** (`BRU-ARB-09`, `SOD-04`) |
| **RP-06** | **A discretionary gesture is recorded as a discretionary gesture**, not disguised as an entitlement (§4.9) |
| **RP-07** | **Consistency beats generosity.** Two customers with the same facts get the same answer, and the policy is what makes that possible |

### 4.2 Cancellation

| Situation | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Customer cancels before the company starts** | Full refund of anything paid. **No charge** |
| **Customer cancels a booked on-site visit before the technician travels** | No charge |
| **Customer cancels after the technician has travelled, or is not present at the agreed slot** | **A visit charge may apply where it was disclosed at booking** — `EN-06`, `RF8` |
| **Customer cancels after diagnosis but before repair** | Diagnostic charge retained **only where disclosed before the diagnosis** (`RF3`, `LG-013` §4.10) |
| **Customer cancels mid-repair** | Work performed and parts consumed are chargeable; the balance is refunded. The device is returned in its current state, **with the state recorded** |
| **Company cancels** — no parts, no capability, no access | **Full refund.** No deduction, and the customer is told why |
| **Company cancels a contract-scheduled visit** | Rescheduled; contract entitlement is not consumed |

### 4.4 Timing — **provisional pending `RF1`, `O-07`**

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Decision on a refund request | **Within 3 business days** of the request and the evidence being complete |
| **Refund executed once approved** | **Within 5 business days**, and **within 10 days of the request in any case where `RF1` applies** |
| Credit note issued | **With the refund, not after it** (`BRU-ARB-09`) |
| Customer told the decision | **Immediately on decision**, with the reason and, where refused, the route in §8 |
| Refused refund | Reason stated in writing, with the escalation route |

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **4.4.1** | **The clock runs from the request, not from internal readiness** |
| **4.4.2** | **A refund the customer has to chase is a complaint the company created** (`BRU-CXM-07`) |
| **4.4.3** | **Any bank or gateway charge on a refund is borne by the company, not deducted from the customer** — the Guidelines' position and the company's in any case |
| **4.4.4** | Where a refund is delayed beyond §4.4, the customer is told **before** the target passes, with a new date |

### 4.5 Method

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **4.5.1** | **A refund is executed only through the treasury function, to a method within the approved set** (`BRU-ARB-11`, `MD-038`) |
| **4.5.2** | **Refund is to the original medium** — bKash to bKash, card to card, bank to bank — **subject to `RF5`**. This is both the Guidelines' position and the correct anti-fraud position |
| **4.5.3** | **Cash refunds are not made above the approval threshold** (`BRU-ARB-11`), and a cash refund is receipted (`DT-016`) |
| **4.5.4** | Where the original method cannot receive a refund, the company refunds to a verified account **in the customer's own name**, with the verification recorded |
| **4.5.5** | **A refund is never made to a third party's account** on the customer's instruction alone |
| **4.5.6** | A refund on an invoiced amount **requires a credit note** (`BRU-ARB-09`), with a reason from the taxonomy (`BRU-ARB-10`), approved by someone other than the requester (`SOD-04`) |
| **4.5.7** | **VAT is adjusted by the credit note** — subject to `RF9`. A refund made without one misstates the VAT position |

### 4.6 Shop returns — **pending `RF4`**

Goods bought through the `P01` shop may be returned within the published return window, unused and
in their original packaging, for a refund. Faulty goods are refunded or replaced at the customer's
choice with return carriage at the company's cost. **Any restocking or handling deduction is
published, applies only to a non-faulty return, and is subject to `RF4`.** Consumables, opened
software and items made or configured to order are excluded from the discretionary return right, but
**never from the faulty-goods right**.

### 4.8 Compensation

Compensation is for loss or inconvenience the company caused. It is **assessed on what the customer
actually lost**, not negotiated on how loudly they complained.

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **4.8.1** | A compensation payment follows an accepted complaint and states what it is for |
| **4.8.2** | It is **not conditional on the customer withdrawing a complaint**, agreeing not to review, or waiving a statutory right |
| **4.8.3** | **A complaint is closed only on the customer's confirmation** (`BRU-QMS-04`, `BRU-CXM-04`) — paying compensation does not close it |
| **4.8.4** | A complaint indicating a process failure raises a non-conformance (`BRU-CXM-06`). **Paying a customer and not fixing the process guarantees paying the next one** |
| **4.8.5** | Compensation is not offered as an alternative to a remedy the customer is entitled to |

### 4.9 Goodwill — **pending `RF6`**

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **4.9.1** | Goodwill is discretionary, **without admission of liability**, and is recorded as goodwill |
| **4.9.2** | It is offered where the company is **not** at fault but the customer's experience was poor — a long wait, a supplier delay, a communication failure |
| **4.9.3** | **It is never used to avoid an entitlement in §4.3.** Offering goodwill where a refund is owed is a policy breach, not a kindness |
| **4.9.4** | Limits are in §4.7 and are **proposals pending `O-02`** |
| **4.9.5** | The wording used is fixed so it does not become an admission — **`RF6`** |
| **4.9.6** | Goodwill in vouchers or points is valued at cost to the company and recorded at that value |

## 5. Liability, Exclusions and Limits

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| **5.1** | **A refund under this policy is not an admission of liability** unless the reason code says the company was at fault |
| **5.2** | This policy does not limit liability. Limits are in `LG-013` §5 and `LG-002` §5 |
| **5.3** | **Nothing here reduces a statutory consumer right** — subject to `RF2` — and §4.8.2 prohibits conditioning a payment on waiving one |
| **5.4** | **The customer's right to complain to the Directorate of National Consumer Rights Protection is preserved and unconditional** |
| **5.5** | Where a refund arises from a counterparty's failure — a seller, a courier, a supplier — the company refunds the customer first and recovers separately (`BRU-MKP-20`), and **its obligation to the customer is not reduced by a recovery it fails to make** |

## 7. Termination and Consequences

| Event | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Refund approved and executed** | Credit note issued; VAT adjusted; the reason recorded; the customer notified |
| **Refund refused** | Reason in writing, escalation route given, complaint route open. **The refusal and its reason are recorded** |
| **Compensation paid** | Recorded with its reason code; **the complaint stays open until the customer confirms** (`BRU-CXM-04`) |
| **A process failure identified** | A non-conformance is raised (`BRU-CXM-06`); **the money is not the fix** |
| **Customer's account closes with a refund outstanding** | The refund is still owed and is executed to a verified account in the customer's name |
| **This policy is superseded** | Requests already made are decided under the version in force when they were made; prior versions retained (`LG-009` `DC-23`) |
