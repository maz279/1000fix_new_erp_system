| Item | Value |

## 1. Purpose

The Master Index states the position with no hedging: *"partners will use the 1000FiX name; the
only choice is whether they do it correctly."*

Five external populations have a commercial reason to say they work with 1000FiX, and each of them
creates a different exposure:

| Population | What they will produce | Exposure |
|---|---|---|
| **Referral and alliance partners** | Emails, landing pages, in-branch material to their own members | A capability promise we have not made (`T-A12`) |
| **Corporate resellers** | Product listings for refurbished stock | A warranty representation that becomes our liability |
| **Franchisees** | Signage, livery, local advertising | A customer cannot distinguish it from an owned branch (`EO-010`) |
| **Brand partners** | Joint statements about authorised service | Their marks and ours in one asset — **two consents, not one** |
| **Marketplace sellers** | Shopfront and social presence | **The one that matters most**: a customer who believes a seller *is* 1000FiX |

`LG-022` `CC-06` names the likeliest origin of the worst instance: *"the corporate website is a
separate content set with a separate approval authority — which is where an overstated claim will
originate."* A partner's asset is the second likeliest, and it has no approval authority at all
unless this guide creates one.

**What this guide is not.** It is not the master brand identity — `VB-001` is voice and tone,
`VB-003` is enterprise UI, `VB-004` and `VB-005` are print and email templates, and **no file in
this content set holds the 1000FiX logo and identity standard** (`O-01`). It is not the agreement
clause (`LG-004`, `LG-005`, `LG-007`), not the campaign execution (`M35`), and not the asset
repository standard (`MM-012`, **written 2026-08-18**; `DA-25` requires a per-partner issue record).

## 4. Content

### 4.1 Who may say what

**The permission is graded, and the grading is the guide's first job.** A marketplace seller and
an alliance partner are not entitled to the same statement, and treating them alike is how a
seller ends up looking like a branch.

| Population | May state | May use the logo | May co-brand | Basis |
|---|---|:--:|:--:|---|
| **Franchisee** | "A 1000FiX franchise branch" — full identity | **Yes**, per the franchise standard | Yes, within the standard | Franchise agreement; `EO-010` §4.4 standard 1 |
| **Alliance partner** | "In partnership with 1000FiX" for the scheme, and only for the scheme | **Yes**, in an approved lock-up | Yes, on approval | `LG-007` §4.7 |
| **Referral partner** | "A 1000FiX referral partner" | **Yes**, in an approved lock-up | On approval | `LG-007` §4.7 |
| **Corporate reseller** | "Authorised reseller of 1000FiX refurbished products" | **Yes**, restricted to the product context | Limited | `LG-007` §4.9 — **pending** |
| **Brand partner** | Joint statements about authorised service | **Both marks, both consents** | Case by case | `LG-005` |
| **Marketplace seller** | **"Available on the 1000FiX marketplace"** — nothing stronger | **No** | **No** | `LG-004`; `EO-002` |

> **The seller row is the one that will be argued and must not move.** A seller displaying the
> 1000FiX logo on their shopfront creates a customer belief that 1000FiX is the counterparty — the
> belief `M12` already concedes exists (*"the customer's counterparty is 1000FiX in their
> perception whatever the agreement says"*) and which `BRU-MKP-20` makes us pay for. **Permitting
> the logo would convert a perception into a representation.** Sellers get a marketplace
> statement, not a mark.

### 4.2 The asset journey

| # | Stage | Who | Service level | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Check entitlement** | Partner | — | §4.1 row confirmed |
| **2** | **Start from a template** | Partner | — | An approved template from `P12`, or a stated reason why none fits |
| **3** | **Submit the request** | Partner via `P12` (`FR-PCM-040`) | — | Asset, audience, channel, run dates, territory, languages |
| **4** | **Brand review** | Brand Lead | 5 working days | Marks, lock-up, templates, tone (`VB-001`) |
| **5** | **Claims review** | Legal + Brand where `T-A13`, `T-A19`, `T-A12` or `T-A16` fires | **10 working days** (`CG-007` G5) | Every claim sourced and dated (`CG-012`) |
| **6** | **Decision** | Brand Lead, with Legal where escalated | — | Approved, approved with changes, or refused with reasons |
| **7** | **Record** | Brand | — | Approval recorded with version, run dates and territory |
| **8** | **Publish** | Partner; execution by `M35` where 1000FiX-run (`FR-PCM-040`) | — | Live |
| **9** | **Fund** | Partner claims MDF where the programme provides it | — | Claim with evidence (`FR-PCM-039`) |
| **10** | **Expire or withdraw** | Brand | — | Run-date expiry, or withdrawal under §4.7 |

**Stage 5 is the constraint.** `CG-007` `SL-03` already records that *"legal review capacity, not
drafting capacity, determines when 61 files can ship"*. A partner campaign entering the same
queue will wait, and a partner who is not told that at stage 1 will publish without approval.
`CB-05` requires the service level to be stated **before** the partner commissions work.

### 4.3 The relationship statement

The single most-used asset element, and the cheapest thing to get right.

| Population | Approved wording |
|---|---|
| Franchisee | *"[Name] — a 1000FiX franchise branch"* |
| Alliance partner | *"[Scheme] in partnership with 1000FiX"* |
| Referral partner | *"[Name], a 1000FiX referral partner"* |
| Corporate reseller | *"[Name], authorised reseller of 1000FiX refurbished products"* |
| Brand partner | *"1000FiX is an authorised service partner for [Brand]"* — **subject to the brand's own approval** |
| Marketplace seller | *"Available on the 1000FiX marketplace"* |

**Prohibited constructions, for every population:**

| Prohibited | Why |
|---|---|
| *"1000FiX [Partner Name]"* — the name used as a prefix | Implies ownership or a division |
| *"Powered by 1000FiX"*, *"1000FiX inside"* | Implies a technology or supply relationship that does not exist |
| *"Official"*, *"exclusive"*, *"only"* without a written grant | `T-A13` comparative and superlative claims |
| *"1000FiX approved"* applied to the partner's **own** products or services | We approve the relationship, not their catalogue |
| Any 1000FiX warranty, SLA or turnaround commitment | `T-A03`, `T-A10` — those are ours to make and `LG-013`, `LG-015` make them |
| Any use of a **brand partner's** mark by a referral partner or seller | Two consents are required and only one exists |

### 4.4 The asset approval checklist

Applied at stage 4 and 6, and published so partners can self-check before submitting.

| # | Check | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does the population's entitlement (§4.1) cover this use? | `CB-01` |
| 2 | Is the relationship statement one of the §4.3 approved forms, unaltered? | `CB-02` |
| 3 | Is the mark reproduced from the supplied file, not redrawn, recoloured or re-proportioned? | `CB-03` |
| 4 | Is clear space, minimum size and background contrast respected? | **`O-01` — no identity standard exists to check against** |
| 5 | Where both marks appear, is the lock-up an approved one? | `CB-04` |
| 6 | **Does every factual claim carry a source and a verification date?** | `CG-012`; `CB-06` |
| 7 | Is any claim comparative or superlative? | **`T-A13` → Legal + Brand** |
| 8 | Is any claim environmental or recycling-related? | **`T-A16` → Sustainability + Legal** |
| 9 | Does any claim promise a capability beyond the specification? | **`T-A12` → product owner + Legal** |
| 10 | Does the asset use another organisation's mark? | **`T-A19` → Brand + Legal; two consents** |
| 11 | Is an ISO or certification claim made, and is the certificate current? | `LG-022`; **a lapsed certificate requires every claim withdrawn** |
| 12 | Does the asset state pricing, a fee or a commission? | **`T-A11` → Finance + Commercial**; `EO-011` governs the values |
| 13 | Does the asset collect personal data? | **`T-A06`, `T-A07` → DPO + Legal**; `LG-008` |

### 4.5 Templates published to partners

`FR-PCM-040` requires approved materials to be published; `04-00` P12 gives partners *"access
approved marketing materials"*. **Pre-approved templates are the mechanism that keeps stage 5 out
of the critical path** — an asset built from a template and not altered skips the claims review
because its claims were reviewed once.

| Template | For | Pre-approved claims |
|---|---|---|
| Relationship badge (digital and print) | All entitled populations | The §4.3 statement only |
| Email footer / signature block | Referral and alliance partners | Statement + link |
| Landing-page module | Alliance partners | Statement + service description drawn from the approved catalogue |
| In-branch or in-store card | Alliance partners, resellers, franchisees | Statement + hotline **16743** |
| Social post frame | All | Statement only |
| Co-branded leaflet | Alliance partners, franchisees | Statement + approved service description |
| Franchise signage specification | Franchisees | Per `EO-010` §4.4 standard 1 |

> **Every template ships in Bengali and English.** The guide is English (`CG-010` §3.2); the
> assets are not, and `VB-009` governs the Bengali typography. **A partner given only English
> templates will translate them themselves**, which is the machine-translation route `CP-05`
> prohibits — recorded as `O-04`.

### 4.6 MDF and co-op funding

| Element | Position | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Allocation | Funds allocated per partner or programme | `FR-PCM-039` |
| Claim | Submitted **with evidence** of the activity and the spend | `FR-PCM-039` |
| **Approval** | **By someone other than the allocator** | **`BRU-PCM-11`** |

| Payment | Instructed to `M52` | `FR-PCM-039` |

### 4.7 Monitoring, withdrawal and takedown

| Situation | Action | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Asset used past its run dates | Withdrawal notice; template access retained | Brand |
| Asset altered after approval | Withdrawal; re-submission required | Brand |
| Unapproved asset in market | Takedown request, with the agreement clause cited | Brand + Partnerships |
| Prohibited claim in market | **Immediate takedown**; `CG-013` escalation | Brand + Legal |
| Marketplace seller displaying the logo | Immediate takedown; **repeated use is an `EO-002` conduct breach** | Marketplace Operations |
| Certificate lapse (ISO or brand authorisation) | **Every asset carrying the claim is withdrawn** | Brand + Quality (`LG-022`) |
| Relationship terminated | All mark use ceases; de-branding per **`EO-014`** | Partnerships + Legal |

**Monitoring is not specified anywhere and this guide cannot create it.** Nothing in `M40`, `M35`
or `M23` watches for unapproved partner marketing, and the realistic detection route is a customer
or a competitor telling us — `O-05`.

### 4.8 The first campaign

The `EO` first-90-days addition, measured in campaigns.

| Campaign | What to review | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| **First** | Did the partner start from a template, or from a blank page? | Whether §4.5 is discoverable in `P12` |
| **First** | How long did stage 5 actually take against the ten-working-day service level? | Whether the legal queue can carry partner work at all |
| **First** | Did any check in §4.4 fail for want of an upstream standard? | `O-01` — check 4 currently has nothing to check against |
| **Second** | Was the Bengali version produced by us or by the partner? | `O-04` |
| **Second** | Did the asset reach market inside its approved run dates and territory? | Whether §4.7 is enforceable in practice |
| **Every** | Assets in market with no approval record | The only measure that matters, and the one nothing currently produces |

### 4.9 Rules

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **CB-01** | Entitlement is **graded by population** (§4.1) and is never generalised. A partner asking for a franchisee's entitlement is refused with the reason |
| **CB-02** | The relationship statement is used **verbatim** from §4.3. Altered wording is a new claim requiring review |
| **CB-03** | Marks are reproduced from supplied files. **Redrawing, recolouring, re-proportioning, outlining or applying effects is prohibited** |
| **CB-04** | A lock-up combining 1000FiX with another mark is approved **as a lock-up**, not as two approved marks placed together |
| **CB-05** | The stage-5 service level is stated to the partner **before** they commission work (§4.2) |
| **CB-06** | Every factual claim carries a source and a verification date (`CG-012`, `CM-R8`). **A claim a partner cannot source is refused, not softened** |
| **CB-07** | Every approval carries **run dates, territory and channels**. An approval without an end date is a permanent grant and is never issued |
| **CB-08** | `CG-013` escalations `T-A19`, `T-A13`, `T-A16`, `T-A12`, `T-A11`, `T-A06` and `T-A07` are applied to partner assets exactly as to our own content |
| **CB-09** | **Marketplace sellers do not use the logo.** The rule is stated with its reason (§4.1) so it survives the argument it will attract |
| **CB-10** | A partner may not use a **brand partner's** mark. Two consents are required; the brand's is not ours to give |
| **CB-11** | The guide is English; **every template it publishes is bilingual**, professionally translated (`CP-05`, `VB-009`) |
| **CB-12** | MDF claims are approved by someone other than the allocator (`BRU-PCM-11`), and the fund ledger is append-only (`T-09`) |
| **CB-13** | Approval decisions that set a precedent are logged in **`RF-006`** |
| **CB-14** | On termination, mark use ceases and de-branding follows `EO-014`. The guide states this at grant, not at exit |
